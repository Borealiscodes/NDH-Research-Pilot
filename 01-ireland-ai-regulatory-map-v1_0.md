### 🇮🇪 Ireland AI Regulatory Map v1.0  
*Regulators • AI Office • Distributed Model*

---

#### 1 — Core AI Oversight Structure

- **AI Office of Ireland (Oifig Náisiúnta na hIntleachta Saorga)**  
  **Role:** Central coordinator and single point of contact (SPOC) for the EU AI Act in Ireland; coordinates ~15 sectoral regulators in a distributed model.   [regulatoryai.eu](https://www.regulatoryai.eu/ai-act-ireland/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/Irish%20Government%20publishes%20General%20Scheme%20of%20AI%20Bill.pdf)  

- **Distributed Model (Competent Authorities)**  
  Ireland uses existing regulators as **AI Act competent authorities**, each supervising AI in its own sector (finance, data, media, utilities, etc.).   [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/Ireland%20Adopts%20Distributed%20Model%20for%20AI%20Oversight.pdf)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/Irish%20Government%20publishes%20General%20Scheme%20of%20AI%20Bill.pdf)

#### 2 — Key AI Regulators (Competent Authorities)

- **Data Protection Commission (DPC)**  
  **Domain:** AI involving personal data, fundamental rights, big‑tech platforms.  
  **AI Role:** Market surveillance authority for certain prohibited practices and high‑risk AI use cases involving personal data.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [regulatoryai.eu](https://www.regulatoryai.eu/ai-act-ireland/)  

- **Central Bank of Ireland (CBI)**  
  **Domain:** Financial services, algorithmic trading, credit scoring, risk models.  
  **AI Role:** Competent authority and market surveillance for high‑risk AI in finance.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Ireland%20Appoints%20Its%20AI%20Act%20Competent%20Authorities%20-%20WILLIAM%20FRY.pdf)  

- **Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC)**  
  **Domain:** Competition, consumer protection, market fairness.  
  **AI Role:** Market surveillance authority for certain high‑risk AI systems affecting consumers.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/Ireland%20Adopts%20Distributed%20Model%20for%20AI%20Oversight.pdf)  

- **ComReg (Commission for Communications Regulation)**  
  **Domain:** Telecoms, electronic communications, network infrastructure.  
  **AI Role:** Competent authority for AI in communications and radio equipment contexts.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/Ireland%20Appoints%20Its%20AI%20Act%20Competent%20Authorities%20-%20WILLIAM%20FRY.pdf)  

- **Coimisiún na Meán**  
  **Domain:** Media, audiovisual services, online platforms.  
  **AI Role:** Competent authority for AI in media and content regulation.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/Ireland%20Adopts%20Distributed%20Model%20for%20AI%20Oversight.pdf)  

- **Commission for Regulation of Utilities (CRU)**  
  **Domain:** Energy and water regulation.  
  **AI Role:** Competent authority for AI in energy and utilities infrastructure.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/Ireland%20Adopts%20Distributed%20Model%20for%20AI%20Oversight.pdf)  

- **Health and Safety Authority (HSA)**  
  **Domain:** Workplace safety, industrial systems.  
  **AI Role:** Market surveillance for AI safety systems linked to harmonised EU product legislation.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/Ireland%20Adopts%20Distributed%20Model%20for%20AI%20Oversight.pdf)  

- **Health Products Regulatory Authority (HPRA)**  
  **Domain:** Medical devices, diagnostics, health products.  
  **AI Role:** Competent authority for AI embedded in medical devices and IVDs.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/Ireland%20Adopts%20Distributed%20Model%20for%20AI%20Oversight.pdf)  

- **Health Service Executive (HSE)**  
  **Domain:** Public health and social care delivery.  
  **AI Role:** Competent authority for operational use of AI in health services.   [aioffice.gov.ie](https://aioffice.gov.ie/national-competent-authorities/)  [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2025-10/Ireland%20Adopts%20Distributed%20Model%20for%20AI%20Oversight.pdf)

#### 3 — Legal & Temporal Context

- **EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)** applies directly in Ireland; no transposition needed.   [regulatoryai.eu](https://www.regulatoryai.eu/ai-act-ireland/)  
- Ireland’s **Regulation of Artificial Intelligence Bill 2026** (General Scheme) implements national supervision and formally establishes the AI Office and distributed oversight model.   [service.betterregulation.com](https://service.betterregulation.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/Irish%20Government%20publishes%20General%20Scheme%20of%20AI%20Bill.pdf)  
- As of mid‑2026, prohibited AI practices and GPAI obligations are already in force; high‑risk obligations phase in through 2027–2028.   [regulatoryai.eu](https://www.regulatoryai.eu/ai-act-ireland/)
