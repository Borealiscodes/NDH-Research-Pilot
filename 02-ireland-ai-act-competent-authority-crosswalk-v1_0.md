## 🏛️ **2 — Core Architecture of AI Oversight in Ireland**

### ⭐ AI Office of Ireland  
Ireland’s central coordinator and **single point of contact (SPOC)** for the EU AI Act.

- Coordinates ~15 sectoral regulators  
- Runs sandboxes, guidance, and national AI governance  
- Acts as a **market surveillance authority** for cross‑sector AI issues  

### 🔗 Distributed Competent Authority Model  
Ireland designates **existing regulators** as AI Act competent authorities within their sectors.

This is the backbone of the crosswalk.

---

## 🗂️ **3 — Crosswalk: Competent Authorities & Domains**

### 🔐 Data Protection Commission (DPC)
- **Role:** Market Surveillance Authority (MSA) for AI involving personal data  
- **Domain:** GDPR, profiling, automated decision‑making  
- **NDH Surface:** Any NDH system touching personal data or rights

### 💶 Central Bank of Ireland (CBI)
- **Role:** Competent authority + MSA for high‑risk AI in finance  
- **Domain:** credit scoring, AML/KYC, algorithmic trading  
- **NDH Surface:** NDH in financial risk engines or compliance

### 🛒 Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC)
- **Role:** MSA for high‑risk consumer‑facing AI  
- **Domain:** recommender systems, pricing algorithms  
- **NDH Surface:** NDH in consumer analytics or market behaviour

### 📡 ComReg
- **Role:** MSA for AI in telecoms + radio equipment  
- **Domain:** network optimisation, routing, communications  
- **NDH Surface:** NDH in telecom infrastructure or digital services

### 📺 Coimisiún na Meán
- **Role:** Competent authority for AI in media  
- **Domain:** content moderation, audiovisual services  
- **NDH Surface:** NDH in ranking, moderation, or media analytics

### ⚡ CRU (Commission for Regulation of Utilities)
- **Role:** Competent authority for AI in energy + utilities  
- **Domain:** grid management, demand prediction  
- **NDH Surface:** NDH in critical infrastructure optimisation

### 🦺 Health and Safety Authority (HSA)
- **Role:** MSA for AI safety systems under harmonised EU product laws  
- **Domain:** industrial safety, workplace automation  
- **NDH Surface:** NDH in safety‑critical industrial contexts

### 🧬 HPRA (Health Products Regulatory Authority)
- **Role:** Competent authority for AI in medical devices  
- **Domain:** diagnostics, clinical support  
- **NDH Surface:** NDH embedded in regulated medical tools

### 🏥 HSE (Health Service Executive)
- **Role:** MSA for high‑risk AI in public health operations  
- **Domain:** triage, emergency systems, resource allocation  
- **NDH Surface:** NDH in hospital operations or public health analytics

### 🚆🚢 Additional Sectoral Authorities
- Commission for Railway Regulation  
- Marine Survey Office  
- National Transport Authority  
- Workplace Relations Commission  

Each acts as a competent authority for AI in its sector.
