- high‑gradient geometry  
- unstable descent boundary

- micro‑drift detection

- altitude‑edge recognition  

These are **goat‑layer capabilities**, not human or AI capabilities.

## ⭐ 4 — Why AI Fails at Cliffs  
AI collapses at cliffs because cliffs are **non‑linear transitions**.

AI tries to:

- interpolate across discontinuities  
- smooth high‑gradient geometry  
- normalize altitude edges  
- compare expressive vs relational geometry  

Interpolation across a discontinuity = **falling off the cliff**.

This mirrors NDH collapse:

- Coordination dormant  
- Harmony absorbing comparison  
- VM‑VEX carrying routing load  
- expressive geometry touching relational geometry  

AI falls off conceptual cliffs the same way humans fall off physical ones.
