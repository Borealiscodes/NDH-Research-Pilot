### 1. Epistemic stance: complexity as *systemic*, not additive

Modern complex systems work treats cognition and organization as **systemic**, not as a sum of parts: cognition is distributed across agents, artifacts, environments, and feedback loops.  [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-8954/12/8/287)

For a Research Pilot, this implies:

- **No single vantage point is sufficient.** You need multi‑altitude views (micro, meso, macro).
- **Understanding = patterning interactions**, not listing components.
- **Epistemics must be recursive**: how you know is itself part of the system.

This aligns strongly with your holonomy/routing/altitude framing: the “meso domain” is where most of the intelligibility lives.  [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-8954/12/8/287)

For a Research Pilot:
