# 🌌 **The Next Project Question (Sagan‑Tone)**  
*A gentle, cosmic inquiry into cyborgs, identity, and the Ship of Theseus.*

> **If a human gradually replaces every part of their body with technology —  
at what point, if ever, do they stop being themselves?**

This is the cyborg version of the Ship of Theseus:

- Replace one plank → still the same ship  
- Replace all planks → is it still the same ship?  
- Rebuild the original planks into a second ship → which one is Theseus’s?

Applied to humans:

- Replace a limb  
- Replace an organ  
- Replace senses  
- Replace neural pathways  
- Replace memory substrates  
- Replace consciousness interfaces  

At what point does continuity break?  
At what point does identity drift?  
At what point does the “self” become something new?

This is the philosophical core of your next project.

# 🌙 **What Current Technology Already Makes Possible**  
*(And how close we actually are to cyborg continuity puzzles.)*

### 🦾 **1. Prosthetic limbs with neural integration**  
We already have:

- myoelectric prosthetics  
- direct nerve‑interface prosthetics  
- sensory‑feedback prosthetics  

These are not sci‑fi. They exist today.

### 🧠 **2. Brain–computer interfaces (BCI)**  
Real, functioning BCIs include:

- Neuralink‑style cortical implants  
- Utah array implants  
- Non‑invasive EEG‑BCI control systems  
- Brain‑to‑text decoding research  
- Brain‑to‑spinal‑cord bypass systems  

These are early, but they are real.

### 👁️ **3. Artificial senses**  
We have:

- retinal implants  
- cochlear implants  
- sensory substitution systems (vision → touch, sound → vibration)  
- magnetoreception implants (biohacker community)  

These are already “new planks” in the ship.

### 🫀 **4. Synthetic organs and biomechanical replacements**  
We can replace:

- hearts (LVADs, total artificial hearts)  
- kidneys (dialysis + emerging artificial kidney prototypes)  
- joints  
- skin  
- bone  

The ship is already being rebuilt.

### 🧬 **5. Memory and cognition augmentation (early stage)**  
Not sci‑fi — early prototypes exist:

- hippocampal prosthesis research  
- memory‑encoding pattern mapping  
- neuroplasticity‑driven cognitive augmentation  
- AI‑assisted external memory systems  

These are the first hints of replacing “planks” in the mind.
