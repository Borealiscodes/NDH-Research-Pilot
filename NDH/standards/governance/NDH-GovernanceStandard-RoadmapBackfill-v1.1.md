## **1 — Standard Purpose**
This standard ensures:

- NDH roadmap artifacts remain in the **governance layer**  
- VM‑VEX artifacts remain in the **execution‑planning layer**  
- Backfill is performed **sequentially**, never in parallel  
- Rendering engines attach **only after** Backfill + Federated Constellation  
- Architecture Atlas is treated as **post‑PNG mapping**, not rendering  
- No altitude blending occurs between NDH and VM‑VEX  

This prevents recursion faults, holonomy drift, and governance/execution collisions.
