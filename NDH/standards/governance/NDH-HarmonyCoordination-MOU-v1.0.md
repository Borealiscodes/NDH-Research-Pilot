# 🌌 **Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) — NDH Harmony, Coordination & Concordance (v1.0)**  
### *Non‑Authority • Altitude‑Neutral • Collapse‑Prevention Charter*

---

## ⭐ **1. Parties to the Understanding**

This Memorandum of Understanding is entered between:

- **NDH‑Harmony** — Clarity Skeleton  
- **NDH‑Coordination** — Routing Skeleton  
- **Concordance** — Alignment Principle  
- **NDH Governance Layer** — Roadmap, Constellation, Gates  
- **VM‑VEX Execution Layer** — Planning, Visibility, Ecology  

These parties do **not** form a hierarchy.  
They form a **co‑sovereign membrane**.

---

## ⭐ **2. Purpose of the MOU**

The purpose of this MOU is to:

- define the relationship between Harmony, Coordination, and Concordance  
- prevent altitude collapse across NDH layers  
- preserve structural coherence  
- maintain routing integrity  
- ensure governance and execution remain separated  
- prevent cognitive, expressive, or structural drift  
- formalize the non‑authority posture of Harmony and Coordination  

This MOU does **not** activate any subsystem.

# ⭐ **4. Shared Commitments**

### **4.1 Non‑Authority Commitment**
All parties agree:

- not to govern  
- not to decide  
- not to interpret cognitive content  
- not to activate runtime systems  
- not to collapse altitudes  
- not to absorb expressive geometry  

Harmony and Coordination remain **structural**, not **directive**.

---

### **4.2 Altitude‑Separation Commitment**
All parties agree to maintain:

- altitude boundaries  
- routing separation  
- non‑dual quarantine  
- expressive geometry dormancy  
- Constellation dormancy  
- Coordination dormancy  

This prevents collapse.

---

### **4.3 Drift‑Routing Commitment**
Harmony detects drift.  
Coordination routes drift.  
Concordance aligns the receiving altitude.

| Drift Type | Harmony Detects | Coordination Routes | Concordance Aligns |
|------------|------------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Practical | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| Conceptual | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| Emotional | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| Ethical | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |
| Structural | ✔️ | ✔️ | ✔️ |

---

### **4.4 Governance‑Execution Separation Commitment**

All parties agree:

- NDH governs **what** happens  
- VM‑VEX governs **how** it happens  
- Harmony + Coordination prevent collapse between them  
- Concordance ensures alignment without authority
