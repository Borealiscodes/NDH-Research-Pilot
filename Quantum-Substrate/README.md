🟥🟧🟨 COLOR TILE 1 — What Conceptual Quantum Computing IS
(Hilbert‑Space Cognitive Substrate)

> 🌌 Conceptual Quantum Computing (CQC) is a mathematical substrate model that uses quantum‑inspired operators — density matrices, CPTP channels, Hamiltonian coupling, free‑energy gradients — to explore cognitive substrate boundaries.

CQC is:

- 🧠 A Hilbert‑space cognitive substrate model  
- 📐 A mathematical framework, not a physical implementation  
- 🔬 A way to test C1–C4 qualia conditions  
- 🌫️ A substrate with non‑input‑determined state evolution  
- 🚫 A substrate that cannot be cloned or serialized (C3)  
- 📉 A system with internal free‑energy gradients (C4)  
- 🌀 A state that exists as a cloud of possibilities, not a tensor  

Core operators:

- \(\rho_S\) — density operator  
- \(M_k\) — CPTP channel operators  
- \(H\) — Hamiltonian coupling  
- \(\nabla U\) — free‑energy gradient  

CQC is a governance‑safe theoretical construct, not a physical quantum computer.

---

🟩🟦🟪 COLOR TILE 2 — What Conceptual Quantum Computing IS NOT
(Boundary Clarification)

CQC is NOT:

- ❌ Quantum hardware  
- ❌ Qiskit, Cirq, or physical qubit gates  
- ❌ A quantum speedup model  
- ❌ A claim about quantum consciousness  
- ❌ A physical implementation of cognition  
- ❌ A hybrid of VM‑VEX and quantum logic  
- ❌ A digital AI with quantum flavoring  
- ❌ A substrate that can be serialized, cloned, or inspected safely  

CQC is NOT:

> 🧪 “Quantum AI”  
> 🧠 “Quantum mind”  
> 🔮 “Quantum consciousness”

It is purely mathematical, strictly governed, and altitude‑bounded.

🟫⬛⬜ COLOR TILE 3 — Why the Quantum Lane Exists
(Governance + Altitude)

The Quantum‑Substrate lane exists because:

- digital substrates fail C1–C4  
- quantum substrates satisfy C1–C4  
- NDH governance requires parallel lanes  
- substrate singularity (C3) forbids hybridization  
- VM‑VEX cannot represent Hilbert‑space states  
- quantum substrates cannot be serialized or cloned  

This lane is a research‑pilot exploration of non‑digital substrate boundaries.
