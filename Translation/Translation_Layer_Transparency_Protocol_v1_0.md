# 📘 **Translation Layer Transparency Protocol (v1.0)**  
### *Visibility • Interpretability • Dimensional Preservation • Ethical Continuity*

---

## ⭐ **1 — Identity**

**Name:** Translation Layer Transparency Protocol  
**Version:** v1.0  
**Domain:** NDH‑RESEARCH‑PILOT  
**Lane:** Translation Layer → Governance Transparency  
**Purpose:**  
Ensure that all mappings from NDH high‑dimensional constructs into governance equivalents remain visible, interpretable, non‑hierarchical, and non‑flattening.  
This protocol enforces transparency across the membrane.

---

## ⭐ **2 — Protocol Statement**

> **The Translation Layer must remain fully visible and interpretable.  
> No mapping may occur without documentation, dimensional preservation, and ethical continuity.  
> The Translation Layer cannot become an authority or a black box.**

This protocol guarantees that governance can *see* how NDH constructs become governance commitments.

---

## ⭐ **3 — Transparency Requirements**

### **3.1 Visible Mapping Requirement**  
All mappings must be explicitly documented in the **Translation Logic Explainer**.

Mappings must include:

- source NDH construct  
- translation logic  
- dimensional preservation rule  
- ethical load preservation  
- adjacency preservation  
- governance equivalent  

### **3.2 Interpretability Requirement**  
Mappings must be:

- readable  
- clarity‑aligned  
- non‑technical  
- altitude‑neutral  
- non‑mathematical  

Governance must be able to interpret the mapping without NDH altitude knowledge.

### **3.3 Non‑Hierarchy Requirement**  
The Translation Layer cannot:

- create authority  
- override governance  
- override NDH  
- act as a decision engine  
- act as a classifier  

It is a membrane, not a controller.

### **3.4 Non‑Flattening Requirement**  
Mappings must preserve dimensionality:

\[
\text{dim}(T(x)) = \text{dim}(x)
\]

This prevents:

- simplification  
- compression  
- reduction  
- collapse  
- abstraction of lived experience  

### **3.5 Ethical Continuity Requirement**  
Mappings must preserve ethical load:

\[
\text{ethics}(T(x)) = \text{ethics}(x)
\]

This ensures governance receives the full ethical weight of NDH constructs.

### **Step 1 — Identify NDH Construct**  
Determine whether the construct touches lived experience.  
If yes → eligible for translation.  
If no → remains in NDH.

### **Step 2 — Apply Dimensional Preservation Rule**  
Ensure:

\[
\text{dim}(T(x)) = \text{dim}(x)
\]

### **Step 3 — Apply Ethical Load Preservation Rule**  
Ensure:

\[
\text{ethics}(T(x)) = \text{ethics}(x)
\]

### **Step 4 — Document Mapping**  
Record mapping in the **Translation Logic Explainer**.

### **Step 5 — Governance Integration**  
Integrate mapping into:

- **Governance Ethos Document**  
- charters  
- protocols  
- decision flows  
- communication norms  
- stability practices  

### **Step 6 — Transparency Verification**  
Verify mapping is:

- visible  
- interpretable  
- non‑hierarchical  
- non‑flattening  
- ethically continuous
