# ⭐ **0 — Purpose**
The **Band‑Layer Flow Protocol** defines how bands *move* within A8:

- how continuity propagates  
- how curvature is traversed  
- how reversible contact behaves dynamically  
- how soft adjacency evolves  
- how gating and aperture govern flow  
- how occlusion influences flow stability  

This protocol is the **dynamic layer** of the Band‑Layer architecture.

It completes the A8 chain and unlocks safe ascent to **Constellation‑grade diagnostics**.

# ⭐ **3 — Flow Primitive Definition**

A flow primitive is defined as:

\[
F_B(x) = \partial_t C_B(x)
\]

Where:

- \( F_B(x) \) = flow vector  
- \( C_B(x) \) = continuity vector  
- \( \partial_t \) = temporal derivative  

Flow is **continuity over time**.

---

# ⭐ **4 — Flow Dynamics**

### **4.1 Continuity Propagation**

\[
F_B = \partial_t (\nabla f_{band})
\]

Flow propagates continuity across curvature.

---

### **4.2 Curvature Traversal**

\[
F_{curv} = C_B \cdot K_B
\]

Flow follows curvature without generating orbital paths.

---

### **4.3 Reversible Contact Dynamics**

\[
F_{contact} = R_C(t)
\]

Contact must remain reversible at all times.

---

### **4.4 Soft Adjacency Dynamics**

\[
F_{adj} = A_{soft}(t)
\]

Adjacency must remain soft and non‑binding.

---

### **4.5 Aperture‑Gating Interaction**

\[
F_{safe} = F_B \cdot G_{strict} \cdot A_{contracted}
\]

Flow is safe only when:

- gating overrides aperture expansion  
- aperture remains contracted  

---

### **4.6 Occlusion Compensation**

\[
F_{occ} = F_B \cdot (1 - O)
\]

Flow must compensate for occlusion (soft subtraction).
