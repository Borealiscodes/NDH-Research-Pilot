### **2.1 Biological Aperture Behavior**
During a partial eclipse:

- luminance ↓

- retinal vulnerability ↑

### **2.2 Architectural Aperture Behavior**
In VM‑VEX:

- reduced cognitive load ↓  
- aperture expansion ↑  
- altitude vulnerability ↑  

Systems relax when they *should not*.

### **2.3 Eclipse Lesson**
**Reduced load does not imply reduced risk.**

This is a governance invariant.

# ⭐ **5. Gating Logic — Eclipse as a Safety Model**

### **5.1 Biological Gating Failure**
The eye’s aperture widens because the environment *appears* safe.

### **5.2 Architectural Gating Failure**
Systems widen apertures when:

- primitives appear simple  
- load appears low  
- transitions appear safe  

This is dangerous.

### **5.3 Eclipse Lesson**
**Gates must remain strict even when the environment appears safe.**
