# ⭐ **2. Diagram Interpretation**

### **Top Node**  
The Omnibus is the **root governance artifact**.

### **Middle Layer**  
The five ethos domains form the **structural pillars**:

- Structural Alignment  
- Altitude Governance  
- Primitive Separation  
- Sequencing Discipline  
- Cognitive Mode  

### **Bottom Layer**  
Each ethos domain expands into **operational principles**:

- foresight, anticipation, stability  
- altitude boundaries, layer seals  
- orb/band/lattice separation  
- precedence ordering, layer closure  
- proactive structural orientation  

This diagram shows how the ethos domains interlock to form a unified architectural stance.
