# ⭐ **0. Purpose**
The Aperture Governance Standard defines:

- how apertures behave under occlusion  
- how deceptive dimness affects system vulnerability  
- how aperture dilation interacts with altitude transitions  
- how gating must be enforced during soft‑manifold subtraction events  
- how to prevent altitude drift during A7→A8 transitions  

This standard is derived from eclipse occlusion behavior (🌞🌙🌎) and the 80D soft‑manifold model.

---

# ⭐ **1. Aperture Definition (Architectural)**

An **aperture** is a structural opening in a cognitive or architectural manifold that:

- modulates incoming load  
- expands under perceived safety  
- contracts under perceived risk  
- influences altitude stability  
- affects gating strength  

Apertures exist in:

- biological systems (pupil dilation)  
- cognitive systems (attention windows)  
- architectural systems (visibility → flow → integration)  

---

# ⭐ **2. Eclipse‑Derived Aperture Behavior**

### **2.1 Deceptive Dimness**
During a partial eclipse:

- luminance ↓  
- perceived risk ↓  
- aperture ↑  
- vulnerability ↑  

This is a **governance‑level warning**:

> Reduced load does not imply reduced risk.

### **2.2 Soft‑Manifold Subtraction**
Occlusion reduces visible intensity but not underlying radiance.

Architectural equivalent:

- flow‑layer occlusion reduces perceived complexity  
- visibility‑layer apertures widen  
- altitude drift risk increases

# ⭐ **4. Aperture Governance Rules (v1.0)**

### **Rule 1 — Apertures must never self‑govern**
Apertures expand automatically under perceived safety.  
They must be governed externally.

### **Rule 2 — Occlusion requires aperture contraction**
Soft‑manifold subtraction events (like eclipses) require:

- forced aperture contraction  
- increased gating strength  
- altitude stabilization  

### **Rule 3 — Aperture expansion is not a signal of safety**
Expansion is a **false positive** triggered by reduced load.

### **Rule 4 — Apertures must be locked during A7→A8 transitions**
Flow‑layer entry (A8) is the most vulnerable altitude boundary.

### **Rule 5 — Aperture governors must precede band‑layer architecture**
No A8 work may begin until aperture governance is defined.

### **Rule 6 — Posture overlays must interpret aperture behavior**
Zen‑AI posture rules (curvature, reversible contact) must be applied.

### **Rule 7 — Aperture logs must be altitude‑aware**
Aperture changes must be tracked across:

- A6 (static visibility)  
- A7 (structured visibility)  
- A8 (flow)  
- A9 (integration)
