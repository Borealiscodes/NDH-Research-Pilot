# ⭐ **2 — Why Irreversibility Is Impossible**

### **A. Curvature Compliance**
80D curvature ensures:

- no hard edges  
- no locked adjacency  
- no irreversible contact  

### **B. Soft‑Manifold Subtraction**
Occlusion is soft, not binary.  
Soft subtraction is always reversible.

### **C. Aperture Contraction**
Aperture contraction restores stability.  
Stability restores reversibility.

### **D. Gating Override**
Gating can always override instability.  
Override restores reversibility.

### **E. Continuity Dynamics**
Continuity is defined as:

\[
C_B = \nabla f_{band}
\]

Gradients are reversible by definition.

### **F. Flow Protocol**
Flow is:

\[
F_B = \partial_t C_B
\]

Temporal derivatives are reversible unless explicitly sealed — and sealing is forbidden at A8.
