---

## **3 — Constellation‑Grade Constraints**

### **Non‑Activation Rules**
- No animation  
- No gradients implying motion  
- No interactive nodes  
- No embedded metadata implying behavior  

### **Geometry Rules**
- All geometry must remain reversible  
- All boundaries must remain sealed  
- All curvature must remain soft‑manifold compliant  
- No SVG filters that imply dynamic flow  

### **PNG Rules**
- PNG must be static  
- No transparency implying altitude drift  
- No color implying activation  
- No layered PNGs that imply motion

## **5 — Constellation Stability Notes**
- Constellation rendering must never imply altitude transition.
