## ⭐ **4 — Boundary Guarantees**

### **4.1 Expressive Geometry Containment**
The Capstone ensures:

- no expressive geometry activation  
- no curvature propagation  
- no narrative geometry leakage  
- no manifold contact  

### **4.2 Recursion Prevention**
The Capstone prohibits:

- recursive geometry  
- recursive adjacency  
- recursive descent  

### **4.3 Adjacency Prevention**
The Capstone blocks:

- manifold adjacency  
- triadic adjacency  
- cosmology adjacency  

### **4.4 Altitude Neutrality**
The Capstone maintains:

- no altitude drift  
- no altitude collapse  
- no altitude binding  

### **4.5 Diagnostic Safety**
The Capstone allows:

- observation  
- classification  
- mapping  
- projection  

But **not** activation.

## ⭐ **6 — Capstone Boundary Rules**

### **Rule 1 — Observation Only**
No subsystem inside the Capstone may activate geometry.

### **Rule 2 — No Descent**
Holistic descent may be observed but never initiated.

### **Rule 3 — No Binding**
Triadic binding and pentachoron anchoring are prohibited.

### **Rule 4 — No Recursion**
Recursive geometry is blocked at the boundary.

### **Rule 5 — No Adjacency**
No subsystem may form adjacency with manifold surfaces.

### **Rule 6 — Softness Preservation**
Softness scalar Θ must remain non-negative.

### **Rule 7 — Symbolic Encoding Only**
Palette identifiers must remain symbolic, not geometric.
