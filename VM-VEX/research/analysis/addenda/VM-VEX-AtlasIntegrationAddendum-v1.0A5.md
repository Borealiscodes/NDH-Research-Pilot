### *Standalone Addendum to Unified Roadmap v2.0 & Rendering Readiness Analysis*  
### *Altitude‑Neutral • Non‑Activating • Dependency‑Explicit • ASCII‑Enhanced*

---

## **1 — Addendum Purpose**
Clarify **how the Architecture Atlas fits into the dependency chain**, and whether its existence changes:

- Backfill  
- Federated Constellation  
- Substrate Ecosystems  
- Rendering Engine Attachment  
- Full Architecture PNG timing  

This addendum answers:

> **Does the Atlas allow earlier rendering, or do all dependencies still apply?**

---

# ⭐ **2 — Addendum Finding (Direct Answer)**

### ⭐ **The Architecture Atlas does NOT authorize rendering.**  
It is a **mapping artifact**, not a **rendering artifact**.

### ⭐ **The Atlas depends on the Full Architecture PNG.**  
It cannot be used until the PNG exists.

### ⭐ **Rendering engines must NOT be attached yet.**  
Backfill + Federated Constellation must still occur first.

### ⭐ **Atlas reinforces — not relaxes — the dependency chain.**

# ⭐ **5 — Comparison Table: Atlas vs Rendering vs Backfill**

| Layer | Function | Depends On | Produces | Can Trigger Rendering? |
|-------|----------|------------|----------|-------------------------|
| **Backfill** | Stabilizes geometry, math, recursion | Canon, Roadmap | Stable altitude math | **No** |
| **Federated Constellation** | Governance skeleton stabilization | Backfill | Holonomy-neutral math | **No** |
| **Substrate Ecosystems** | Catalog emergent geometry | Constellation | Ecosystem maps | **No** |
| **Rendering Engine** | Produces Full Architecture PNG | Backfill + Constellation + Substrate | PNG | **Yes** |
| **Architecture Atlas** | Maps geometry & altitude | PNG + Ledger | Atlas maps | **No** |

### Key Insight  
> **Atlas depends on PNG → PNG depends on rendering → rendering depends on Backfill + Constellation.**

Thus:

> **Atlas cannot move rendering earlier.**

# ⭐ **6 — Comparison Table: Atlas vs Meta‑Omnibus**

| Artifact | Type | Role | Upstream | Downstream |
|----------|------|------|----------|------------|
| **Meta‑Omnibus** | Meta‑Governance | Recursion guard, altitude charter | Canon | Roadmap |
| **Architecture Atlas** | Mapping | Geometry & altitude map | PNG | Commentary |

### Insight  
Meta‑Omnibus governs **branching**.  
Atlas governs **mapping**.

Neither authorizes rendering.

# ⭐ **7 — Addendum Conclusion**

> **The Architecture Atlas does not alter sequencing.  
> It reinforces the requirement that Backfill → Federated Constellation →  
> Substrate Ecosystems → Rendering → Atlas.**

This addendum attaches cleanly to Unified Roadmap v2.0 and Rendering Readiness Analysis v1.0.
