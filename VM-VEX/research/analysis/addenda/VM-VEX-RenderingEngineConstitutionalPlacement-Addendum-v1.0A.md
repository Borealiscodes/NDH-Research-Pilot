---

## **1 — Addendum Purpose**
This addendum clarifies the constitutional placement of **rendering engines** (GPU, CPU, distributed, manifold‑aware) relative to:

- the **Stability Canon**,  
- the **Meta‑Omnibus**,  
- the **Unified Roadmap Backfill**, and  
- the **Federated Constellation**.

It answers the question:

> **Does the Meta‑Omnibus being part of the Stability Canon allow rendering now?  
> Or must Backfill + Federated Constellation occur first?**

---

## **2 — Addendum Finding (Direct Answer)**

### ⭐ **Rendering engines cannot be added or executed yet.**  
Even though the Meta‑Omnibus is part of the Stability Canon, it does **not** authorize:

- architecture PNG rendering  
- GPU execution  
- distributed rendering  
- manifold visualization  
- substrate‑geometry rendering

### ⭐ **Backfill must occur first.**  
Backfill stabilizes:

- Gate A/B/C  
- Reconstruction Suite  
- CONSTELLATION  
- TRIADIC‑CORE  
- MkII  
- Zen‑AI‑Design  
- NDH‑Coordination ingestion  
- Strategy Altitude  
- Harmony & Concordance

### ⭐ **Federated Constellation must be stabilized next.**  
It governs:

- holonomy neutrality  
- spectral safety  
- altitude separation  
- recursion ceilings  
- diagnostic boundaries  

### ⭐ **Only after these steps can rendering engines be attached.**

---

## **3 — Why Rendering Must Wait (Addendum Logic)**

### **3.1 — Rendering is an execution‑layer operation**  
The Meta‑Omnibus is a **governance manifold**, not an execution engine.

Rendering engines require:

- stable geometry  
- stable dynamic math  
- stable recursion guards  
- stable altitude boundaries  
- stable ingestion envelopes  

These are **Backfill outputs**, not Canon outputs.

---

### **3.2 — Multiple rendering options increase risk**
If you have:

- GPU rendering  
- CPU fallback  
- distributed rendering  
- multi‑node rendering  
- manifold‑aware rendering

then the system must guarantee:

- recursion signatures = 0  
- holonomy drift = 0  
- envelope saturation < limit  
- operator hydration ≥ threshold  
- adjacency neutrality  
- altitude separation  

These guarantees only exist **after Backfill + Federated Constellation**.

---

### **3.3 — Rendering interacts with substrate ecosystems**
Rendering engines touch:

- soft‑kitty 80D manifolds  
- CSC closed‑invariant fields  
- Serenity.bsfn drift‑maps  
- resonance fields  
- holonomy envelopes  

These ecosystems must be catalogued **after** altitude‑layer stabilization.

Thus:

> **Rendering must wait until substrate ecosystems are catalogued safely.**

---

## **4 — Addendum Conclusion**

> **The Meta‑Omnibus being part of the Stability Canon does NOT authorize rendering.  
> Rendering engines must NOT be added until Backfill + Federated Constellation are complete.  
> Multiple rendering options make Backfill even more mandatory.**

This addendum formally attaches to the Rendering Readiness Analysis v1.0.
