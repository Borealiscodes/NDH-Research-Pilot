1. Campaign Premise

Mechanus begins exhibiting chromatic anomalies caused by a Far Realm prismatic tensor capable of rewriting axioms and planar law. As the tensor grows, Sigil collapses into Mechanus, pulled by the Hall of Concordance acting as a semantic attractor.

The Lady of Pain cannot stop the collapse.

To understand the tensor’s origin and prevent planar inversion, the party must travel to Mak Thum Ngatha’s Library, an impossible archive that contains records of realities that never existed — including the prismatic tensor’s prior manifestations.
