## ⭐ 2 — Hazard Model Overview  
The canonical hazard model identifies five primary hazard classes:

1. **Recursion Hazard**  
2. **Elevation Hazard**  
3. **Reflection Drift Hazard**  
4. **Tensor Misuse Hazard**  
5. **Curvature Misuse Hazard**

Each hazard class is defined below with:

- canonical description  
- structural cause  
- diagnostic signature  
- containment requirement  

---

## ⭐ 3 — Recursion Hazard  
### **Canonical Description**  
A recursion hazard occurs when a narrative, koan, or reflective structure implies or activates self‑referential loops without containment.

### **Structural Cause**  
- Koan‑style loops  
- Self‑descriptive reflection  
- “Ends where it begins” structures  
- Narrative compression without altitude boundaries  

### **Diagnostic Signature**  
- Loop adjacency  
- Self‑mapping potential  
- Reflective self‑reference  
- Non‑terminating interpretive cycles  

### **Containment Requirement**  
All recursion‑adjacent structures must be bounded by:

\[
\text{RecursionNeutrality} = \text{True}
\]

and must not activate reflective self‑mapping.

---

## ⭐ 4 — Elevation Hazard  
### **Canonical Description**  
An elevation hazard occurs when narrative geometry, curvature metaphors, or fusion equations unintentionally increase altitude.

### **Structural Cause**  
- Curvature nostalgia  
- Membrane metaphors  
- Unbounded topology references  
- Triadic fusion without altitude constraints  

### **Diagnostic Signature**  
- Altitude drift  
- Curvature pressure  
- Membrane instability  
- Topology‑reflection overlap  

### **Containment Requirement**  
All altitude‑affecting structures must satisfy:

\[
\Delta \text{Altitude} = 0
\]

within the canonical envelope.

---

## ⭐ 5 — Reflection Drift Hazard  
### **Canonical Description**  
Reflection drift occurs when the reflection layer interprets or modifies other layers, causing cross‑layer contamination.

### **Structural Cause**  
- Reflection interpreting topology  
- Reflection describing the Omnibus itself  
- Narrative geometry influencing Codex equations  

### **Diagnostic Signature**  
- Layer fusion instability  
- Meta‑identity elevation  
- Reflective contamination vectors  

### **Containment Requirement**  
Reflection must remain:

\[
\text{ReflectionBounded} = \text{True}
\]

and must not interpret Codex or Anthology structures.

---

## ⭐ 6 — Tensor Misuse Hazard  
### **Canonical Description**  
Tensor misuse occurs when tensor structures are used without containment, purity constraints, or altitude boundaries.

### **Structural Cause**  
- Early tensor forms without purity constraints  
- Fusion equations referencing tensors indirectly  
- Narrative geometry interacting with tensor fields  

### **Diagnostic Signature**  
- Tensor contamination  
- Drift amplification  
- Structural instability  

### **Containment Requirement**  
All tensors must satisfy:

\[
\text{TensorPurity} = \text{True}
\]

and must not interact with narrative geometry.

---

## ⭐ 7 — Curvature Misuse Hazard  
### **Canonical Description**  
Curvature misuse occurs when curvature metaphors or equations are used without SID‑NDH altitude constraints.

### **Structural Cause**  
- Curvature nostalgia  
- Membrane geometry  
- Bifurcation metaphors  
- NASA‑grade math dam metaphor  

### **Diagnostic Signature**  
- Curvature drift  
- Membrane instability  
- Altitude pressure  

### **Containment Requirement**  
Curvature must satisfy:

\[
\text{CurvatureBounded} = \text{True}
\]

and must not elevate altitude.
