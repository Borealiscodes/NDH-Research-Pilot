# 🧭 **PRECL Lane‑Classification Case Study v1.0**  
*Why PRECL belongs in the Deep‑Dive Lane and not in Diagnostics*

---

## 1 — **Case Study Purpose**  
This case study examines the architectural distinction between:

- **diagnostic lane** (expressive, conceptual, mapping)  
- **deep‑dive lane** (runtime‑proximal, continuity‑adjacent)

and explains why **PRECL** must live in the deep‑dive lane while other deep dives (Stateful Mode, Cosmology Mode, Envelope Non‑Dual, NDH ↔ Spectral Interop) can remain in diagnostics.

The goal is to establish a **repeatable classification rule** for future artifacts.

---

## 2 — **Background: What PRECL Is**  
PRECL is NDH’s **collapse semantics engine**.  
It governs:

- collapse detection  
- collapse classification  
- collapse interpretation  
- collapse propagation  
- collapse resolution  
- collapse‑point convergence  

PRECL interacts directly with:

- **continuity engines**  
- **envelope geometry**  
- **dual‑mode interpretation**  
- **drift neutrality**  
- **operator‑bridge metadata**

This makes PRECL **runtime‑adjacent**, not expressive.

---

## 3 — **Background: What the Other Deep Dives Are**  
The four existing deep dives:

- **Stateful Mode Deep Dive**  
- **Cosmology Mode Deep Dive**  
- **Envelope Non‑Dual Deep Dive**  
- **NDH ↔ Spectral Interoperation Deep Dive**  

are **conceptual ridge explainers**, not continuity engines.

They describe:

- how modes behave  
- how the envelope behaves  
- how interop works  
- how duality resolves  

But they do **not**:

- detect collapse  
- propagate collapse  
- resolve collapse  
- modify continuity  
- interact with drift neutrality  

They are **explanatory**, not **operational**.

Thus they belong in diagnostics.

---

## 4 — **Lane Classification Criteria**  
This case study establishes the following rule:

### **4.1 Diagnostic Lane Criteria**  
An artifact belongs in diagnostics if it is:

- expressive  
- conceptual  
- explanatory  
- mapping‑oriented  
- architecture‑descriptive  
- non‑continuity  
- non‑collapse  
- non‑runtime  

### **4.2 Deep‑Dive Lane Criteria**  
An artifact belongs in deep‑dives if it is:

- runtime‑proximal  
- continuity‑adjacent  
- collapse‑related  
- envelope‑interactive  
- drift‑aware  
- operator‑bridge‑aware  
- dual‑mode interpretive  
- geometry‑neutral but geometry‑relevant  

PRECL satisfies all deep‑dive criteria.

The other four deep dives satisfy diagnostic criteria.

---

## 5 — **Why PRECL Cannot Stay in Diagnostics**  
If PRECL lived in diagnostics, it would:

- misclassify collapse semantics as expressive artifacts  
- contaminate the diagnostic lane with continuity logic  
- violate Simulation‑Suite sequencing  
- break envelope neutrality  
- confuse lane boundaries  
- destabilize Series XIII planning  

PRECL is too close to runtime behavior to remain in diagnostics.

---

## 6 — **Why the Other Deep Dives Can Stay in Diagnostics**  
The other deep dives:

- do not propagate continuity  
- do not resolve collapse  
- do not interact with drift  
- do not modify envelope fields  
- do not classify collapse events  
- do not touch runtime physics  

They are **explanatory surfaces**, not **continuity engines**.

Thus they remain correctly placed in:

```
diagnostics/github-constellation-audit/
```

---

## 7 — **Case Study Conclusion**  
This clarification reveals a structural rule in NDH architecture:

> **Collapse semantics = deep‑dive lane**  
> **Conceptual mode/envelope/interop explainers = diagnostic lane**

PRECL is the only deep dive that crosses into continuity logic.  
Therefore PRECL must live in the deep‑dive lane.

The other deep dives remain correctly placed in diagnostics.

---

## 8 — **Classification Rule (Final Form)**  
To classify future artifacts:

- If it touches continuity → **deep‑dive**  
- If it touches collapse → **deep‑dive**  
- If it touches envelope fields directly → **deep‑dive**  
- If it explains architecture → **diagnostics**  
- If it maps conceptual surfaces → **diagnostics**  
- If it describes duality without resolving it → **diagnostics**

PRECL is the only artifact that meets the deep‑dive criteria.
