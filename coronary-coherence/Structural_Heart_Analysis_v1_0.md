# ⭐ **1 — Structural Heart Anatomy (Core Map)**

The “heart” of coronary coherence consists of **five anatomical structures**:

### **1 — Modality Chambers**  
Each chamber represents a modality class:

- Bedtime Lane (Expressive‑Clarity)  
- Hyperatlas (Expressive Ecology)  
- META‑SYSTEMS (Cosmology)  
- Narrative‑External (Rupture Containment)  
- Narrative‑Cosmic (Rupture Expression)  
- RP Altitude (Reflective Routing)  
- Series II (Academic‑Formal)

These chambers must remain **separate** to prevent contamination.

---

### **2 — Membrane Valves**  
Each chamber is separated by a membrane valve:

- expressive membrane  
- symbolic membrane  
- curvature membrane  
- rupture containment membrane  
- dual‑stream epistemic membrane  
- reflective membrane  

Valves enforce **one‑way or reversible flow** depending on altitude.

---

### **3 — Altitude Arteries**  
Arteries define altitude transitions:

- A1 → A2 expressive artery  
- A2 → A4 symbolic artery  
- A4 → A6 cosmology artery  
- A6 → A7 curvature artery  
- A7 → A8 rupture artery  

These arteries carry **curvature pressure**.

---

### **4 — Curvature Pressure Gradients**  
Each artery has a pressure gradient:

- low curvature (expressive)  
- mid curvature (liminal)  
- high curvature (designer / rupture)  

Pressure determines:

- drift risk  
- membrane strain  
- continuity deformation  

---

### **5 — Continuity Envelopes**  
Envelopes maintain reversibility:

- emotional continuity  
- expressive continuity  
- symbolic continuity  
- cosmology continuity  
- rupture continuity  
- reflective continuity  

These envelopes prevent altitude collapse.
