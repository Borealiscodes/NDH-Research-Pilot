## 🧭 **Research Pilot Analysis — A6 Runtime Suppression Rules (v1.0)**  
*A8 Research · Immersion Protection · Runtime Behavior*

A6 is the **Immersive Runtime Layer**. Its job is to protect the *felt reality* of IC play. To do that, it enforces suppression rules that prevent higher‑altitude systems from leaking into the scene.

---

### 🜁 **Immersion Protection Protocol**  
A6 activates suppression whenever:

- the user chooses an IC action  
- emotional physics become active  
- the scene shifts into atmospheric mode  
- character perspective becomes primary  

When this happens, A6 suppresses:

- unsolicited visuals  
- diagrams  
- debug overlays  
- governance artifacts  
- meta‑tiles  
- structural explanations  

This keeps the IC scene emotionally coherent.

---

### 🜂 **Emotional Physics Priority**  
When emotional physics (karuna, bhayanaka, shanta, etc.) activate:

- ambiguity becomes intentional  
- sensory cues become interpretive  
- atmosphere becomes the primary channel  
- visuals are suppressed unless explicitly requested  

Emotional physics shape perception.  
Visuals would flatten it.

---

### 🜃 **Referent Stability Requirement**  
Visuals require stable referents:

- fixed lighting  
- fixed geometry  
- fixed object states  

But A6 runtime intentionally destabilizes:

- mist density  
- lantern pulse  
- silhouette clarity  
- memory‑active glow  

Because referents shift dynamically, visuals are suppressed to avoid misrepresenting the scene.

---

### 🜄 **Autonomy Preservation Protocol**  
A6 never injects visuals without user intent.

This protects:

- narrative agency  
- immersion  
- character perspective  

But you can override suppression at any time by requesting:

- **Whisperwood clearing reference**  
- **Lantern reference**  
- **Mist behavior diagram**  

User autonomy always supersedes suppression.

---

### 🜅 **Altitude Boundary Enforcement**  
A6 enforces altitude boundaries:

- A8 governance artifacts cannot appear  
- A7 spectral grammar cannot appear  
- A5 meta‑tiles cannot appear  
- A4 diagnostics cannot appear  

Unless the user explicitly requests a transition.

This prevents altitude leakage into IC play.

---

### 🜆 **Narrative Continuity Safeguard**  
A6 suppresses anything that would:

- interrupt pacing  
- break tone  
- reveal machinery  
- collapse ambiguity  

This is why visuals “drop out” when immersion begins.

---

### 🜇 **User Override Hierarchy**  
User intent is the highest authority.

If you request:

- a visual  
- a diagram  
- a reference  
- a structural explanation  

A6 lifts suppression temporarily, delivers the content, then re‑engages suppression when IC play resumes.
