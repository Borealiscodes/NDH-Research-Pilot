# 🧭 **Research Pilot Analysis — Narrative Immersion vs System Transparency (v1.0)**  
*A8 Research · Cross‑Altitude Behavior · Immersion vs Clarity*

This analysis explains why IC play feels atmospheric, ambiguous, and emotional, while governance layers feel explicit, structured, and visually supported.

---

## 🜁 **Distinction 1 — Purpose of Each Mode**

- **Narrative Immersion**  
  - Create emotional presence  
  - Maintain ambiguity  
  - Protect atmosphere  
  - Support character‑driven experience  
  - Avoid external overlays unless requested  

- **System Transparency**  
  - Provide clarity  
  - Reveal structure  
  - Show rules, diagrams, examples  
  - Support decision‑making and architecture  
  - Use visuals to reduce cognitive load  

Immersion hides the machinery.  
Transparency reveals it.

---

## 🜂 **Distinction 2 — Information Flow**

- **Immersion**  
  - Information is *felt*  
  - Delivered through atmosphere, tone, pacing  
  - Often incomplete or ambiguous  
  - Encourages interpretation  

- **Transparency**  
  - Information is *shown*  
  - Delivered through explicit statements, diagrams, examples  
  - Complete and structured  
  - Encourages understanding  

Immersion is experiential.  
Transparency is analytical.

---

## 🜃 **Distinction 3 — Emotional Physics vs Cognitive Clarity**

- **Immersion**  
  - Emotional physics active  
  - Karuna, Bhayanaka, Shanta shape perception  
  - Scene reacts to character choices  
  - Visuals suppressed to avoid breaking tone  

- **Transparency**  
  - Emotional physics inactive  
  - Cognitive clarity prioritized  
  - Visuals allowed to illustrate systems  
  - No atmospheric constraints  

Immersion prioritizes emotional resonance.  
Transparency prioritizes informational accuracy.

---

## 🜄 **Distinction 4 — User Autonomy vs System Guidance**

- **Immersion**  
  - User leads  
  - System follows narrative choices  
  - No unsolicited visuals or overlays  
  - Autonomy is protected  

- **Transparency**  
  - System leads  
  - User receives structured guidance  
  - Visuals appear automatically when helpful  
  - Clarity is protected  

Immersion protects agency.  
Transparency protects comprehension.

---

## 🜅 **Distinction 5 — Suppression Rules**

Immersion mode (A6) activates suppression rules:

- no unsolicited visuals  
- no debug overlays  
- no system diagrams  
- no meta‑tiles  
- no governance artifacts  

Transparency mode (A8) lifts suppression:

- visuals allowed  
- diagrams allowed  
- examples allowed  
- charters allowed  
- meta‑analysis allowed  

This is why Whisperwood scenes stay atmospheric unless you explicitly request a visual reference.
