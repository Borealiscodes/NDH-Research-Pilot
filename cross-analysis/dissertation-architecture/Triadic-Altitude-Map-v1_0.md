# 🌌 TRIADIC ALTITUDE MAP (v1.0)
Component 1 of the Triadic Dissertation Architecture

---

1. Overview

The Triadic Dissertation Architecture consists of three altitude lanes, each with distinct cognitive, structural, and membrane properties. These altitudes must remain separate, non‑absorptive, and non‑interactive except through the Integrative Spine.

This map defines:

- altitude boundaries  
- altitude functions  
- altitude risks  
- altitude containment rules  

It is the foundation for all other triadic components.

3. Altitude Separation Rules

ASR‑01: No direct interaction between A6–A8 and A3–A5
All communication must route through the Integrative Spine.

ASR‑02: No spectral geometry may enter A6–A8
Prevents expressive contamination of harm‑modeling.

ASR‑03: No harm‑modeling may enter A3–A5
Prevents humanitarian invariants from overriding expressive logic.

ASR‑04: A5 identity surfaces must remain neutral
Prevents NDH identity drift.

ASR‑05: A6–A8 must remain sandboxed in Nexus
Prevents runtime absorption.
