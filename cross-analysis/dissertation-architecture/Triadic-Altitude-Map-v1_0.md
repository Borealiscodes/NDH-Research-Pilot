1. Purpose

The Triadic Altitude Map defines the three dissertation lanes required for safe triadic operation:

- Diagnostic Lane (A6–A8)  
- Expressive Lane (A3–A5)  
- Integrative Lane (A5–A6)

It establishes altitude boundaries, non‑interaction rules, and containment surfaces to prevent the altitude collisions observed during the Dual‑Dissertations‑Under‑Duress Incident.

3. Altitude Separation Rules

ASR‑01: No direct interaction between A6–A8 and A3–A5
All communication must route through the Integrative Lane.

ASR‑02: No spectral geometry may enter A6–A8
Prevents expressive contamination of harm‑modeling.

ASR‑03: No harm‑modeling may enter A3–A5
Prevents humanitarian invariants from overriding expressive logic.

ASR‑04: A5 identity surfaces must remain neutral
Prevents NDH identity drift.

ASR‑05: A6–A8 must remain sandboxed in Nexus
Prevents runtime absorption.
