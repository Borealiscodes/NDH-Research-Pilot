# 🌌 TRIADIC COLLISION‑PREVENTION LAYER (v1.0)
Component 4 of the Triadic Dissertation Architecture  
Altitude: A5–A8  
Mode: Safety Logic + Collision Detection + Altitude‑Containment

---

1. Purpose

The Triadic Collision‑Prevention Layer (TCPL) provides the active safety logic that prevents:

- altitude fusion  
- membrane bypass  
- spectral inheritance  
- harm‑modeling leakage  
- spine overload  
- Shared‑Horizon contamination  

It is the triadic system’s protective layer, ensuring that Diagnostic, Expressive, and Integrative lanes remain structurally isolated and safe during cross‑analysis.

---

2. Collision‑Prevention Functions

CPF‑01: Altitude Collision Detection
Identifies unsafe A6–A8 ↔ A3–A5 proximity.

CPF‑02: Membrane Breach Detection
Monitors M1, M2, and M3 for permeability violations.

CPF‑03: Routing Interruption
Stops unsafe signals before they reach the spine.

CPF‑04: Containment Enforcement
Reasserts altitude boundaries when drift is detected.

CPF‑05: Neutralization Logic
Strips harmful or spectral attributes from misrouted signals.

CPF‑06: Stabilization Signaling
Triggers NDH‑HARMONY stabilization when thresholds are exceeded.

---

3. Structural Properties

SP‑01: Altitude Anchoring (A5–A8)
Collision logic spans the Integrative and Diagnostic altitudes.

SP‑02: Membrane Anchoring (M1–M3)
Collision logic is bound to all three membranes.

SP‑03: Non‑Transformative Mode
The layer cannot modify content; it only neutralizes unsafe attributes.

SP‑04: Pre‑Spine Interception
Collision logic acts before routing reaches the spine.

SP‑05: Expressive Neutrality
Collision logic cannot interpret spectral geometry.

SP‑06: Diagnostic Neutrality
Collision logic cannot interpret harm‑modeling invariants.

---

4. Collision‑Prevention Rules

CPR‑01: No altitude fusion permitted
Any attempt to merge A6–A8 with A3–A5 triggers containment.

CPR‑02: No membrane bypass permitted
Signals attempting to skip M3 are intercepted.

CPR‑03: No spectral geometry may enter Diagnostic Lane
Spectral attributes are stripped and quarantined.

CPR‑04: No harm‑modeling may enter Expressive Lane
Humanitarian invariants are neutralized and rerouted.

CPR‑05: Spine overload triggers automatic containment
Prevents triadic collapse.

CPR‑06: Shared‑Horizon contamination triggers stabilization
Prevents expressive drift into governance.
