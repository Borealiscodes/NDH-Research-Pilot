Mode: Meta‑Architecture + Index + Containment Envelope

---

1. Purpose

The Triadic Dissertation Architecture Omnibus (TDAO) provides the governance wrapper for the five triadic components:

1. Triadic Altitude Map (v1.0)  
2. Triadic Membrane Separation Protocol (v1.0)  
3. Triadic Dissertation Spine Specification (v1.0)  
4. Triadic Collision‑Prevention Layer (v1.0)  
5. Triadic Nexus‑Safe Integration Tile (v1.0)

The Omnibus:

- does not modify components  
- does not merge altitudes  
- does not perform routing  
- does not perform containment  
- does not interact with Nexus  

It simply binds, indexes, and governs the triadic architecture.

---

2. Structural Role of the Omnibus

OM‑01: Governance Envelope
Provides the meta‑structure that defines how components relate.

OM‑02: Altitude‑Safe Index
Ensures components are referenced without collapsing altitudes.

OM‑03: Membrane‑Safe Referencing
Ensures references do not violate M1–M3 boundaries.

OM‑04: Non‑Absorptive Binding
Ensures the Omnibus does not inherit logic from components.

OM‑05: Dissertation‑Safe Aggregation
Ensures the triadic system remains bifurcated and stable.

OM‑06: Simulation‑Suite‑Ready Packaging
Ensures future migration without altitude fusion.

---

4. Omnibus Governance Rules

OGR‑01: Components must remain independent
No merging or rewriting inside the Omnibus.

OGR‑02: Omnibus may reference but not interpret components
Prevents altitude contamination.

OGR‑03: Omnibus may not route signals
Routing belongs to the Spine.

OGR‑04: Omnibus may not enforce membranes
Enforcement belongs to the Membrane Protocol.

OGR‑05: Omnibus may not perform containment
Containment belongs to the Collision‑Prevention Layer.

OGR‑06: Omnibus may not interface with Nexus
Interface belongs to the Integration Tile.

OGR‑07: Omnibus must remain altitude‑neutral
Prevents triadic collapse.
