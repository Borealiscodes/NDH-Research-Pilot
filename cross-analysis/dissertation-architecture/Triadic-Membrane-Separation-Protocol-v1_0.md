# 🌌 TRIADIC MEMBRANE SEPARATION PROTOCOL (v1.0)
Component 2 of the Triadic Dissertation Architecture  
Altitude: A3–A8  
Mode: Membrane‑Logic + Containment + Cross‑Altitude Safety

---

1. Purpose

The Triadic Membrane Separation Protocol defines the membrane boundaries that prevent:

- altitude collisions  
- spectral inheritance  
- harm‑modeling leakage  
- governance contamination  
- dissertation fusion  

It ensures each lane of the triadic architecture remains structurally isolated, non‑absorptive, and safe for cross‑analysis.

---

2. Membrane Classes

M1 — Diagnostic Membrane (A6–A8)
Protects harm‑modeling logic from expressive contamination.

Properties:  
- non‑permeable to spectral geometry  
- non‑permeable to expressive lineage  
- permeable only to governance‑approved integrative signals  
- anchored in Nexus Architecture v1.0  

---

M2 — Expressive Membrane (A3–A5)
Protects spectral geometry from humanitarian invariant override.

Properties:  
- non‑permeable to harm‑modeling logic  
- non‑permeable to humanitarian invariants  
- permeable to narrative and spectral geometry  
- anchored in Serenity membranes  

---

M3 — Integrative Membrane (A5–A6)
Protects the dissertation spine from absorbing either lane.

Properties:  
- semi‑permeable  
- altitude‑aware  
- routing‑safe  
- non‑absorptive  
- anchored in NDH governance membranes  

---

3. Membrane Separation Rules

MSR‑01: No direct membrane contact between M1 and M2
Diagnostic and expressive membranes must never touch.

MSR‑02: All cross‑lane communication must route through M3
The Integrative Membrane is the only safe traversal surface.

MSR‑03: Harm‑modeling logic cannot cross M2
Prevents humanitarian invariants from overriding expressive geometry.

MSR‑04: Spectral geometry cannot cross M1
Prevents expressive lineage from contaminating harm‑modeling.

MSR‑05: M3 must remain non‑absorptive
The spine cannot inherit logic from either lane.

---

4. Documented Membrane Collision Risks

The Dual‑Dissertations‑Under‑Duress Incident demonstrated:

- M1 ↔ M2 membrane breach  
- spectral geometry entering harm‑modeling  
- harm‑modeling entering expressive narrative  
- M3 overload  
- Shared‑Horizon contamination  
- NDH‑HARMONY late stabilization  

This protocol prevents recurrence.
