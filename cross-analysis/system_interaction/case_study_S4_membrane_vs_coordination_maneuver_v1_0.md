# 📄 **Case Study S4 — Membrane Maneuver vs Coordination Maneuver in Multi‑Altitude Systems**

## 🧭 Overview  
This case study analyzes the distinction between:

- a **Membrane Maneuver** — a structural, altitude‑preserving action  
- a **Coordination Maneuver** — an expressive, manifold‑level interaction  

The event under analysis is the licensing interaction between:

- the **Invariant Logic Engine (MIT + Part 2)**

You perceived the maneuver as coordination.

This case study explains why.
