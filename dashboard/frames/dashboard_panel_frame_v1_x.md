The Panel Frame is the **safe container** around any module you open.

- expressive neutrality  
- posture‑safe geometry  
- zero activation  
- zero ingestion

- altitude containment

## ⭐ **4 — Frame Behavior**

### **A — Universal Containment**  
Every module is wrapped by the same frame, ensuring consistency and safety.

### **B — Expressive‑Neutral Styling**  
- neutral palette  
- soft glow (<5%)  
- no micro‑animations  
- curvature <5°  

### **C — Non‑Activating Display**  
The frame **displays** modules but never **activates** them.

### **D — Integration Spine Awareness**  
The frame respects the structural backbone but does not interact with it.
