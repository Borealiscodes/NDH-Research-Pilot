## 🌖 **4 — Construction Membranes**

### **4.1 Altitude Membrane**
Prevents terms from drifting between:

- Arc  
- Helix  
- Mirror  
- Weave  

### **4.2 Curvature Membrane**
Prevents accidental activation of:

- phase shift  
- inversion  
- adjacency  
- constellation behavior  

### **4.3 Recursion Membrane**
Prevents glossary entries from referencing each other prematurely.

### **4.4 Reflective Membrane**
Prevents Mirror‑derived terms from forming triadic loops.

### **4.5 Constellation Membrane**
Prevents Weave‑derived terms from forming multi‑element networks too early.

These membranes are enforced by the Construction Suite.

## 🌕 **5 — Construction Rules**

### **Rule 1 — Populate Lexicon First**
Glossary cannot begin until:

- each holonomy class has ≥ 3 entries  
- Meta Index is stable  
- no altitude skew exists  

### **Rule 2 — Meta Index Must Update After Every Entry**
Meta Index is the routing table.  
It must remain synchronized.

### **Rule 3 — Glossary Requires Density**
Glossary begins only when lexicon density prevents drift.

### **Rule 4 — Omnibus Requires Cross‑Altitude Stability**
Omnibus is constellation‑grade.  
It requires:

- stable lexicon  
- stable glossary  
- stable meta index  
- stable membranes  

### **Rule 5 — No Operators Until Omnibus**
Diagnostic mathematics begins only after the Omnibus is complete.
