## **1 — Identity Statement**

The **Borealiscodes GitHub constellation** is a multi‑layer ecosystem of 38 repositories spanning cognitive architecture, spectral geometry, humane design, governance ecology, expressive grammars, and experimental sandboxes.  

It is not a single project.  
It is a **constellation** — a distributed architecture composed of distinct clusters that together express a coherent design philosophy:

- humane systems  
- spectral reasoning  
- non‑authority governance  
- ND‑safe expressive surfaces  
- runtime clarity  
- architectural dignity  

The constellation is defined not by a central runtime, but by a **shared ethos**:  
gentle, reversible, sovereignty‑neutral design.

## **2 — Constellation Purpose**

The constellation exists to explore and demonstrate:

- cognitive geometry  
- spectral engines  
- humane AI design  
- governance membranes  
- expressive‑layer architectures  
- trauma‑informed systems  
- multi‑altitude reasoning  
- prismatic conceptual tools  

It is both **research** and **architecture**, but not production infrastructure.

## **3 — Constellation Shape (Topological Overview)**

The constellation forms **five major clusters**, each with its own internal logic:

1. **NDH Core Cluster**  
   Cognitive architecture, governance, coordination, startup, harmony, platforms.

2. **Spectral Geometry Cluster**  
   Spectral runtimes, spectral pedagogy, spectral imagination, spectral publication.

3. **Rainbows / Expressive Layer Cluster**  
   humane design, prismatic layers, Shared‑Horizon, Zen‑AI, trauma‑informed systems.

4. **Governance Ecology Cluster**  
   treaties, charters, goat‑inspired ecological governance, non‑authority structures.

5. **Experimental / Sandbox Cluster**  
   prototypes, tests, narrative surfaces, early‑stage explorations.

These clusters are **not hierarchical**.  
They are **parallel surfaces** expressing different aspects of the same design ethos.
