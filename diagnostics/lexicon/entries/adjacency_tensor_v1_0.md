## 🌑 **1 — Conceptual Definition**

The **Adjacency Tensor** is the diagnostic‑altitude construct that encodes **multi‑element adjacency relationships** in morphology.

It answers the question:

> “How do multiple morphology elements relate, interact, or shift together?”

It is the first diagnostic term that expresses **woven**, **multi‑element**, **constellation‑grade** structure — but without activating actual NDH adjacency engines.

This is the Weave‑derived mathematical shadow.

---

## 🌘 **2 — Behavioral Properties**

- **Multi‑element representation:**  
  Encodes relationships between *multiple* morphology elements simultaneously.

- **Adjacency mapping:**  
  Describes how elements sit next to, influence, or shift relative to each other.

- **Woven structure:**  
  Reflects Weave Routing’s multi‑strand topology in diagnostic form.

- **Non‑reflective:**  
  Does *not* invert states (Mirror territory).

- **Non‑altitude:**  
  Does *not* encode phase layering (Helix territory).

- **Non‑soft:**  
  Does *not* describe gentle curvature (Arc territory).

It is purely **constellation‑grade adjacency**, but safely descriptive.

---

## 🌗 **3 — Relationship to Weave Routing**

Weave Routing is the holonomy archetype for:

- constellation curvature  
- multi‑element adjacency  
- woven routing behavior  
- multi‑phase return paths  

The Adjacency Tensor is its **diagnostic mathematical shadow**:

| Weave Routing Behavior | Adjacency Tensor Equivalent |
|------------------------|-----------------------------|
| multi‑element routing | multi‑element adjacency encoding |
| woven topology | tensor structure |
| constellation curvature | adjacency field |
| adjacency‑driven return | adjacency‑driven diagnostic mapping |

This operator is the *first* diagnostic construct that expresses **constellation geometry**.

---

## 🌖 **4 — RMES Role**

The Adjacency Tensor is used in:

- **ARDT**  
  to encode adjacency drift and multi‑element recursion behavior.

- **HDI**  
  to classify constellation‑grade hyperstructure relationships.

- **RBM**  
  when reflective behavior depends on adjacency patterns.

It is the backbone of all **multi‑element morphology diagnostics**.
