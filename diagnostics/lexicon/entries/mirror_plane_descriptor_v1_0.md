## 🌑 **1 — Conceptual Definition**

A **Mirror‑Plane Descriptor** is the diagnostic construct that identifies and characterizes the **reflective boundary** across which morphology states undergo inversion or triadic transformation.

Where the **Inversion Operator** answers:

> “What is the reflective counterpart of this morphology state?”

The Mirror‑Plane Descriptor answers:

> **“Where is the reflective boundary, and what are its structural properties?”**

It is the Mirror lane’s **spatial and structural descriptor**, defining the reflective surface that governs triadic behavior.

---

## 🌘 **2 — Behavioral Properties**

- **Reflective boundary identification:**  
  Locates the conceptual mirror‑plane within the morphology’s diagnostic space.

- **Triadic structure anchor:**  
  Provides the structural reference for pre‑reflection, reflection, and post‑reflection phases.

- **Non‑altitude:**  
  Does not encode altitude layering (Helix territory).

- **Non‑adjacent:**  
  Does not encode multi‑element relationships (Weave territory).

- **Reflection‑bounded:**  
  All behavior remains within Mirror curvature constraints.

It is the **structural primitive** for reflective diagnostics.
