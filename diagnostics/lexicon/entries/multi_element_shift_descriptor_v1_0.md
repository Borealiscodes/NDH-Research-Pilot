## 🌑 **1 — Conceptual Definition**

A **Multi‑Element Shift Descriptor** is the diagnostic construct describing **how multiple morphology elements shift relative to one another** within constellation‑grade structure, without activating adjacency engines or hyperstructure traversal.

Where the **Woven Morphology State** answers:

> “What is the morphology *doing* when woven across multiple elements?”

The Multi‑Element Shift Descriptor answers:

> **“How do the elements *move* relative to each other within that woven configuration?”**

It is the Weave lane’s **dynamic descriptor**, capturing multi‑element motion in expressive‑clarity form.

---

## 🌘 **2 — Behavioral Properties**

- **Relative multi‑element motion:**  
  Encodes how elements shift, slide, rotate, or reposition within a constellation structure.

- **Constellation‑bounded:**  
  All behavior remains within Weave curvature constraints.

- **Non‑reflective:**  
  Does not encode mirror‑plane inversion (Mirror territory).

- **Non‑altitude:**  
  Does not encode layered phase behavior (Helix territory).

- **Non‑soft:**  
  Does not encode gentle curvature (Arc territory).

It is the **motion counterpart** to the Woven Morphology State.

---

Weave Routing governs:

## 🌖 **4 — RMES Role**

Multi‑Element Shift Descriptor is used in:
