## 🌑 **1 — Conceptual Definition**

An **Orientation Shift Descriptor** is the Arc‑derived diagnostic construct that describes **directional changes** in a morphology’s orientation while remaining within soft‑curvature boundaries.

Where **Soft Morphology State** describes *what* the morphology is doing,  
the Orientation Shift Descriptor describes:

> **How the morphology’s orientation changes while remaining gentle, stable, and non‑altitude.**

It is the Arc‑lane’s **directional behavior descriptor**, sitting between soft curvature and altitude curvature.

---

## 🌘 **2 — Behavioral Properties**

- **Directional deviation:**  
  Captures small, stable changes in orientation (tilt, lean, rotation) without invoking altitude phases.

- **Non‑altitude:**  
  Does not encode layered phase behavior (Helix territory).

- **Non‑reflective:**  
  Does not encode mirror‑plane inversion (Mirror territory).

- **Non‑adjacent:**  
  Does not encode multi‑element relationships (Weave territory).

- **Soft‑bounded:**  
  All orientation changes remain within Arc curvature limits.

It is the **orientation counterpart** to the Soft Morphology State.

---

## 🌗 **3 — Relationship to Arc Routing**

Arc Routing governs:

- gentle curvature  
- stable deviation  
- non‑spiral behavior  
- non‑reflective behavior  

Orientation Shift Descriptor is the **directional shadow** of Arc Routing:

| Arc Behavior | Orientation Shift Descriptor Equivalent |
|--------------|------------------------------------------|
| gentle bend | gentle orientation change |
| stable arc | stable directional deviation |
| non‑spiral | non‑altitude orientation |
| non‑reflective | non‑mirror orientation |

This descriptor is essential for soft‑curvature diagnostics.

---

## 🌖 **4 — RMES Role**

Orientation Shift Descriptor is used in:

- **MOP** — orientation classification  
- **RBM** — pre‑phase directional context  
- **ARDT** — orientation drift detection  
- **HDI** — orientation mapping in soft‑state hyperstructure  

It is the **directional diagnostic primitive** for Arc‑derived morphology.
