## 🌑 **1 — Conceptual Definition**

A **Soft Morphology State** is the baseline morphology descriptor for **gentle, non‑altitude, non‑reflective, non‑adjacent** configurations.  
It is the *state‑level counterpart* to the **Soft Curvature Scalar**.

Where the scalar answers:

> “How softly bent is this?”

The Soft Morphology State answers:

> “What is the morphology *doing* while it is softly bent?”

It is the **Arc‑derived morphology state**, sitting at the lowest curvature altitude.

---

## 🌘 **2 — Behavioral Properties**

- **Baseline morphology:**  
  Represents the morphology before altitude, reflection, or adjacency effects.

- **Gentle deviation:**  
  Encodes small, stable, non‑spiral, non‑inverted changes.

- **State‑level descriptor:**  
  Unlike the scalar (numeric), this is **qualitative** and **structural**.

- **Non‑phase:**  
  Does not encode altitude phases (Helix territory).

- **Non‑reflective:**  
  Does not encode mirror‑plane behavior (Mirror territory).

- **Non‑adjacent:**  
  Does not encode multi‑element relationships (Weave territory).

It is the **purest morphology state** in the diagnostic language.

---

## 🌗 **3 — Relationship to Arc Routing**

Arc Routing is the holonomy archetype for:

- gentle curvature  
- stable deviation  
- soft routing  
- non‑reflective, non‑adjacent behavior  

Soft Morphology State is the **diagnostic morphology shadow** of Arc Routing:

| Arc Behavior | Soft Morphology State Equivalent |
|--------------|----------------------------------|
| gentle bend | gentle morphology deviation |
| stable arc | stable soft morphology |
| non‑spiral | non‑altitude state |
| non‑reflective | non‑mirror state |

This is the foundational morphology state for all Arc‑derived diagnostics.

---

## 🌖 **4 — RMES Role**

Soft Morphology State is used in:

- **MOP** — baseline morphology classification  
- **RBM** — pre‑phase morphology context  
- **ARDT** — soft‑state drift detection  
- **HDI** — soft‑state hyperstructure mapping  

It is the **starting point** for all morphology diagnostics.
