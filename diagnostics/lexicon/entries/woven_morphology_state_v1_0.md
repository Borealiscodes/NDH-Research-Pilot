## 🌑 **1 — Conceptual Definition**

A **Woven Morphology State** is the diagnostic construct describing morphology configurations that exhibit **multi‑element, constellation‑grade structural relationships** without invoking adjacency engines or constellation traversal.

Where the **Adjacency Tensor** answers:

> “How do multiple elements relate mathematically?”

The Woven Morphology State answers:

> **“What is the morphology *doing* when its structure is woven across multiple elements?”**

It is the Weave lane’s **state‑level descriptor**, capturing constellation‑grade structure in expressive‑clarity form.

---

## 🌘 **2 — Behavioral Properties**

- **Multi‑element structure:**  
  Represents morphology states involving multiple interacting elements.

- **Constellation‑grade behavior:**  
  Encodes woven structure without activating adjacency or hyperstructure engines.

- **Non‑reflective:**  
  Does not encode mirror‑plane inversion (Mirror territory).

- **Non‑altitude:**  
  Does not encode layered phase behavior (Helix territory).

- **Non‑soft:**  
  Does not encode gentle curvature (Arc territory).

It is the **state counterpart** to the Adjacency Tensor.

## 🌗 **3 — Relationship to Weave Routing**

Weave Routing governs:

- constellation curvature
