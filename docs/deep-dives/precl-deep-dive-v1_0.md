# 🌀 **PRECL Deep Dive v1.0**  
*NDH Collapse Semantics — Unified Interpretation Across Dual Runtime Modes*

---

## 1. **Definition and Scope**  
**PRECL** (Pre‑Collapse Logic) is NDH’s unified collapse mechanism.  
It governs how collapse events are:

- detected  
- classified  
- interpreted  
- propagated  
- resolved  

across both NDH runtime modes:

- **Stateful Mode** (physical continuity)  
- **Cosmology Mode** (expressive continuity)

PRECL is **dual‑interpretive** but **single‑geometric**:  
collapse is interpreted differently depending on mode, but the envelope geometry remains non‑dual.

---

## 2. **PRECL’s Position in NDH Architecture**  
PRECL sits between:

- **runtime continuity engines**  
- **envelope geometry**  
- **operator grammar**  

It is not:

- a physics engine  
- a narrative engine  
- a solver  
- a spectral operator  
- a drift regulator  

It is a **collapse interpreter**.

PRECL ensures that collapse events do not:

- break continuity  
- destabilize envelope geometry  
- violate drift neutrality  
- contaminate operator grammar  
- create dual envelopes  

---

## 3. **PRECL Event Lifecycle**  
A PRECL event follows a strict lifecycle:

### 3.1 **Detection**  
Collapse is detected when one or more envelope fields undergo discontinuity:

- **adjacency**  
- **resonance**  
- **flow**  
- **holonomy**  
- **density**  
- **synthesis**  

Detection is mode‑agnostic.

### 3.2 **Classification**  
PRECL classifies collapse into one of two categories:

- **physical collapse** (Stateful Mode)  
- **symbolic collapse** (Cosmology Mode)

### 3.3 **Interpretation**  
Interpretation is mode‑dependent:

- **Stateful Mode:** collapse affects physical continuity  
- **Cosmology Mode:** collapse affects expressive continuity  

### 3.4 **Propagation**  
PRECL propagates collapse through envelope fields without altering envelope geometry.

### 3.5 **Resolution**  
Collapse resolves at the **collapse point**, where dual interpretations converge into a single geometric state.

---

## 4. **PRECL in Stateful Mode**  
In **Stateful Mode**, PRECL is treated as a **physical continuity event**.

Effects include:

- density redistribution  
- adjacency re‑mapping  
- flow perturbation  
- holonomy recalibration  
- synthesis re‑evaluation  

PRECL is logged and becomes part of the continuity record.

PRECL **cannot**:

- break continuity  
- create new geometry  
- alter envelope invariants  

---

## 5. **PRECL in Cosmology Mode**  
In **Cosmology Mode**, PRECL is treated as a **symbolic continuity event**.

Effects include:

- motif emergence  
- expressive curvature shifts  
- narrative adjacency changes  
- symbolic density modulation  
- thematic synthesis  

PRECL is not logged physically; it is interpreted symbolically.

PRECL **cannot**:

- override expressive drift rules  
- create narrative discontinuity  
- alter envelope geometry  

---

## 6. **Envelope Interaction**  
PRECL interacts with envelope fields but **never modifies the envelope itself**.

Envelope remains:

- non‑dual  
- mode‑agnostic  
- geometry‑neutral  
- collapse‑stable  

PRECL is **interpreted through** the envelope, not **applied to** it.

---

## 7. **Collapse Point Semantics**  
The **collapse point** is the geometric location where:

- dual interpretations converge  
- continuity is preserved  
- envelope invariants remain intact  
- collapse resolves into a single geometric state  

It is the anchor that prevents:

- dual envelopes  
- continuity fragmentation  
- drift destabilization  

---

## 8. **Interop Behavior**  
PRECL interacts with **Spectral‑Base Runtime** only through the operator bridge.

Spectral‑Base provides:

- math  
- stability primitives  
- adjacency maps  

NDH provides:

- dual interpretation  
- envelope geometry  
- collapse semantics  

PRECL **never** crosses the bridge as narrative or physics — only as collapse metadata.

---

## 9. **Drift Neutrality**  
PRECL must obey **Drift Neutrality**:

- no collapse may induce uncontrolled drift  
- collapse propagation must remain bounded  
- collapse cannot amplify expressive curvature  
- collapse cannot destabilize physical continuity  

PRECL is drift‑aware but not drift‑driven.

---

PRECL Deep Dive v1.0 is:

- descriptive  
- pedagogical  
- non‑activating  
- reversible  
- runtime‑safe  

It does not:

- activate geometry

- engage sealed layers
