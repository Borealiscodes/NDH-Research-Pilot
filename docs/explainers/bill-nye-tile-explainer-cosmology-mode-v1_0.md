# 🌌🧪 **Bill Nye Tile Explainer — Cosmology Mode Edition**  
*A friendly science‑guy explainer for NDH’s expressive continuity engine.*

---

## 🎬 **Opening Scene**  
*Bill Nye slides onto the tile wearing a star‑patterned blazer and holding a cosmic paintbrush.*

“Hi explorers! Today we’re learning about **Cosmology Mode**, the part of NDH where the universe stops acting like physics and starts acting like **story geometry**!”

He twirls the brush.

“In Stateful Mode, the universe remembers everything.  
In Cosmology Mode, the universe says:  
**‘Let’s pretend!’**”

---

## 🌈 **Bill Nye’s Big Idea**  
Cosmology Mode is NDH’s **expressive continuity engine**.

Bill flips a tile:

> “Continuity is *simulated*, not *stored*.”

Another tile:

> “PRECL collapse becomes *symbolic*, not *physical*.”

A third:

> “Curvature becomes *narrative*, not *geometric*.”

He grins.

“This is the mode where the universe gets poetic.”

---

## 🧬 **Bill Nye’s Science Breakdown**  
Bill draws the envelope fields again:

- adjacency  
- resonance  
- flow  
- holonomy  
- density  
- synthesis  

Then he circles them dramatically.

“In Cosmology Mode, these fields don’t behave like physics — they behave like **storytelling tools**!”

He taps each one:

- **Adjacency** → “Who’s emotionally close?”  
- **Resonance** → “Who’s vibing with the theme?”  
- **Flow** → “Where’s the plot moving?”  
- **Holonomy** → “What happens when the story loops?”  
- **Density** → “How heavy is the moment?”  
- **Synthesis** → “What motifs pop out?”

He slaps a sticker on the board:

> **Continuity: SIMULATED**

---

## 🎨 **Bill Nye Demonstrates Expressive Continuity**  
Bill paints a little comet on the tile.

“In Stateful Mode, this comet has a trajectory.”

He wipes it away with a flourish.

“In Cosmology Mode, the comet has a **vibe**.”

He paints it again, but now it’s rainbow‑colored.

“It’s not about physics — it’s about **expressive curvature**.”

---

## ⚠️ **Bill Nye’s Safety Warning: Soft Drift**  
Bill puts on a glittery helmet.

“In physics land, drift is dangerous.  
But in story land, drift is **fun**!”

He shows a tile labeled:

> **Drift Neutrality: SOFT**

“Cosmology Mode lets the universe wiggle — as long as the wiggle makes sense narratively.”

---

## 🌀 **Bill Nye’s PRECL Demo**  
Bill drops a toy planet.

It collapses into a tiny marble.

“In Stateful Mode, this collapse gets logged.”

He picks up the marble and shakes it like a magic 8‑ball.

“In Cosmology Mode, collapse becomes **symbolism**.  
It’s a metaphor.  
A motif.  
A narrative beat.”

He tosses the marble into a jar labeled:

> **PRECL (Symbolic)**

---

## 🧠 **Bill Nye’s Summary Tile**  
He flips the final tile:

> **Cosmology Mode = Story + Symbolism + Soft Drift + Simulated Continuity + Expressive Curvature**

He gives a thumbs‑up.

“Stateful Mode is physics.  
Cosmology Mode is poetry.  
Same envelope — different vibe.”
