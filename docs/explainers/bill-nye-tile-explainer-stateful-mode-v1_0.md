# 🧪 **Bill Nye Tile Explainer — Stateful Mode Edition**  
*A friendly science‑guy explainer for NDH’s physics half.*





---

## 🎬 **Opening Scene**  
*Bill Nye bursts onto the NDH Tile, wearing a lab coat covered in adjacency‑field doodles.*

“Hi kids! Today we’re learning about **Stateful Mode**, the part of NDH where things actually **stay put**, **move correctly**, and **don’t forget what happened five seconds ago**!”

He taps the envelope diagram.

“This is your **manifold**. Think of it like a cosmic whiteboard. And Stateful Mode is the rule that says:  
**‘Don’t erase the board every time you draw something!’**”

---

## 🔭 **Bill Nye’s Big Idea**  
Stateful Mode is NDH’s **physics engine**.

Bill flips a tile:

> “If you push something, it remembers being pushed.”

He flips another:

> “If something collapses (PRECL), the universe logs it.”

He flips a third:

> “If you walk in a loop, the curvature tells you what changed.”

This is **physical continuity**, not pretend continuity.

---

## 🧬 **Bill Nye’s Science Breakdown**  
Bill draws six fields on the board:

- **Adjacency field** — “Who’s next to who?”  
- **Resonance field** — “Who’s vibrating with who?”  
- **Flow field** — “Where’s stuff moving?”  
- **Holonomy field** — “What happens when you walk in circles?”  
- **Density field** — “How heavy is the situation?”  
- **Synthesis field** — “What do the operators spit out?”

Then he circles them dramatically.

“In Stateful Mode, these fields **change over time**.  
Not symbolically.  
Not narratively.  
But **physically**.”

He slaps a sticker on the board:

> **Persistence: FULL**

---

## 🧲 **Bill Nye Demonstrates Reversibility**  
Bill rolls a ball across the tile.

“Watch this! In Stateful Mode, I can roll the ball forward…”

He rewinds the tape.

“…and I can roll it back!”

He winks.

“Because **reversibility** is required.  
No cheating.  
No narrative shortcuts.  
No ‘oops I forgot what happened.’”

---

## ⚠️ **Bill Nye’s Safety Warning: Drift Neutrality**  
Bill puts on goggles.

“In physics land, drift is dangerous.  
If the manifold starts drifting, you get cosmic wobble!”

He shows a tile labeled:

> **Drift Neutrality: STRICT**

“Stateful Mode clamps drift so the universe doesn’t fall over.”

---

## 🧪 **Bill Nye’s PRECL Demo**  
Bill drops a toy star.

It collapses into a tiny marble.

“That’s a **PRECL event** — a collapse!”

He picks up the marble.

“In Stateful Mode, we **log** this collapse.  
We **accumulate** it.  
We **remember** it.”

He tosses the marble into a labeled jar:

> **PRECL Buffer**

---

## 🧠 **Bill Nye’s Summary Tile**  
He flips the final tile:

> **Stateful Mode = Physics + Memory + Reversibility + Strict Drift + Real PRECL**

He gives a thumbs‑up.

“Cosmology Mode is fun and expressive, but Stateful Mode is where NDH actually behaves like a universe.”
