# **NDH‑Research‑Pilot Draft v1.2**  
### *Exploratory Layer — Mixed Altitude, Pre‑Formal, Pre‑Routing*

## **1. Purpose**

NDH‑Research‑Pilot is NDH’s exploratory layer — the place where ideas first appear in their raw, entangled, altitude‑drifting form.  
Artifacts here are intentionally chaotic:

- semantic fragments  
- algebraic hints  
- geometric intuition  
- proto‑simulation impulses  
- incomplete provenance  
- substrate‑adjacent behavior  

This layer is not meant to be clean.  
It is meant to explore.

---

## **2. Typical Artifact Behavior**

Artifacts produced in Research‑Pilot frequently contain:

- semantic framing mixed with algebra  
- algebra mixed with simulation intent  
- simulation intent mixed with geometry  
- geometry mixed with substrate intuition  
- provenance mixed with exploratory metadata  

These artifacts do **not** respect altitude boundaries.  
They are not decomposed.  
They are not routed.  
They are not stabilized.

They are raw.

---

## **3. Known Issues**

Research‑Pilot regularly exhibits:

- altitude drift  
- subsystem contamination  
- semantic overreach  
- algebraic leakage  
- simulation pre‑activation  
- provenance omission  
- substrate entanglement  

These issues are expected.  
They are part of the layer’s nature.

---

## **4. Why This Layer Exists**

Research‑Pilot is the “first spark” layer.  
It is where NDH discovers new structures before it knows what they are.

It is not meant to be:

- formal  
- stable  
- decomposed  
- routed  
- altitude‑clean  

It is meant to be exploratory.

---

## **5. Relationship to NDH‑Coordination**

Research‑Pilot produces mixed artifacts.  
Coordination decomposes them.

Research‑Pilot drifts.  
Coordination stabilizes.

Research‑Pilot entangles altitudes.  
Coordination separates them.

This relationship is essential.
