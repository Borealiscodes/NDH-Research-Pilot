- “higher”  
- “lower”

### **3.2 Manifold‑Native**
Altitude bands emerge from:

- curvature behavior  
- membrane responsiveness  
- holonomy modulation  
- continuity envelopes  

They are **geometric**, not structural.

### **3.3 Reversible**
Movement between domains is:

- reversible  
- non‑linear

### **3.4 Constellation‑Federated**
Domains are stabilized by:

- distributed reflection

No domain governs another.
