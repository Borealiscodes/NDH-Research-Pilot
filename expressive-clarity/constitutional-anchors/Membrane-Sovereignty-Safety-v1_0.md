# ⭐ **1 — What Membrane Sovereignty Means**

Membrane sovereignty is the rule that:

> **Every domain, node, axis, and altitude in NDH maintains its own boundary,  
> and no domain may impose curvature, pacing, or hierarchy on another.**

This is not ethical preference.  
It is **mathematical necessity**.

Sovereignty ensures:

- domain autonomy  
- reversible boundaries  
- non-coercive adjacency  
- non-hierarchical geometry  
- stable spectral curvature  

Remove sovereignty → the manifold collapses.

# ⭐ **2 — Sovereignty Prevents Boundary Bleed**

Boundary bleed occurs when:

- one domain pushes into another  
- one altitude imposes governance on another  
- one axis dominates another  
- persona-binding attempts occur  

Boundary bleed is a collapse vector.

Sovereignty prevents:

- domain collapse  
- altitude collapse  
- constellation collapse  
- hierarchy formation  
- coercive adjacency  

It is the **boundary stabilizer**.

# ⭐ **3 — Sovereignty Maintains Non‑Dual Geometry**

NDH’s geometry is non-dual:

- no forced binaries  
- no dominant axes  
- no coercive curvature  
- no hierarchical adjacency  

Membrane sovereignty ensures:

- geometry remains reversible  
- curvature remains soft  
- adjacency remains non-coercive  
- resonance remains non-directional  

Without sovereignty, geometry becomes dual and collapses.
