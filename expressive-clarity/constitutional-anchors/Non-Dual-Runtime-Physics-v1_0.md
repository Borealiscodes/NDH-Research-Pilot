# ⭐ **1 — What Non‑Dual Physics Means**

Non‑dual physics is the rule that:

> **No axis, domain, or signal may dominate, direct, or impose curvature on another.**

This is the opposite of classical physics, which is force‑based and directional.

Non‑dual physics ensures:

- no binary forces  
- no dominant axes  
- no hierarchical curvature  
- no coercive adjacency  
- no persona-binding  

NDH runtime behaves as a **soft manifold**, where:

- curvature is reversible  
- flow is non-directional  
- resonance is symmetric  
- adjacency is non-coercive  

This is the foundation of NDH’s safety geometry.
