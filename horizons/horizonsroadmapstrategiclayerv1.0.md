# Long‑Range Research Trajectories for Geometric, Thermodynamic, and Dignity‑Aligned AI

NDH‑RESEARCH‑PILOT — Strategic Horizons Document (v1.0)
Borealis S. Hedling

---

Abstract

This Horizons Roadmap defines the long‑range research trajectories for NDH‑RESEARCH‑PILOT. It integrates geometric reasoning (Voronoi regions, effort‑weighted geodesics, holonomy), thermodynamic feasibility, climate‑aligned computation, and dignity‑aligned governance. The roadmap is structured across three horizons—near, mid, and far—and uses the Direction Manifold (Document 2) as the organizing geometry. Atmospheric reflections highlight the lived reality of climate change and the ethical stakes of AI development. Comparison tables contrast edge‑aligned transparent computation with current opaque datacenter AI. This document completes the three‑layer architecture of the NDH‑RESEARCH‑PILOT.

Objectives

- Effort‑coherence metrics

Horizon II — Thermodynamic & Edge‑Aligned AI (3–7 years)

Replace opaque datacenter inference with transparent, energy‑bounded computation.

Objectives

- flop‑auditable inference  
- entropy‑bounded reasoning  
- reversible or partially reversible CPU inference  
- thermodynamic scheduling  
- edge‑first model architectures  
- geodesic‑aligned reasoning modules  

Expected Outcomes

- dramatic reduction in energy cost  
- transparent thermodynamic profiles  
- climate‑aligned inference pathways  

---

Horizon III — Dignity‑Aligned AI & Societal Integration (7–15 years)

Embed human rights, agency, and planetary limits into AI governance.

Objectives

- non‑absorptive reasoning  
- identity‑safe inference  
- holonomy‑controlled reasoning  
- region‑respecting semantic interaction  
- rights‑aligned computational constraints  
- planetary thermodynamic limits integrated into AI policy  

Expected Outcomes

- AI systems that preserve human agency  
- computation aligned with climate reality  
- governance grounded in geometric transparency

3. Climate Reality (Atmospheric Section)
(Attenborough‑tone, without naming him.)

There are moments when the world speaks without words.  
A rising tide against a stone pier.  
A summer that arrives too early and leaves too late.  
A storm that forms where storms did not form before.

The planet is not abstract.  
It is not a backdrop.  
It is the substrate on which every computation rests.

Every datacenter hum is a small pulse of heat into a system already strained.  
Every opaque model is a shadow cast across a warming world.  
Every unbounded inference is a gesture made without regard for the air that carries it.

There is another way.

Computation can be gentle.  
Inference can be transparent.  
Reasoning can be aligned with the rhythms of a world that has carried life for billions of years.

A Horizons Roadmap is not only a plan.  
It is a promise:  
that the systems we build will belong to the world they inhabit,  
and not stand apart from it.
