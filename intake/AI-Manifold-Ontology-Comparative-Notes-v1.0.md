# ⭐ 1 — Soft Manifold Geometry  
### *Closest match in current research:*  
**Neurosymbolic grounding + multi‑layer ontologies**.

Recent work describes AI systems as layered mappings between context, features, representations, and decisions — essentially a **multi‑dimensional manifold of transformations**. Badea & Gilpin formalize this as a meta‑decision function composed of sequential mappings (Relevance → Representation → Reasoning).   [Emergent Mind](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/artificial-intelligence-ontology-aio)

This is not called “soft manifold geometry,” but the math is similar:  
a pliable, multi‑layer space where meaning is shaped by transformations rather than fixed categories.

# ⭐ 2 — Ontology Blankets  
### *Closest match:*  
**Ontology‑first AI architecture** and **Active Ontology Design**.

Ontology‑first AI explicitly wraps AI systems in a conceptual layer that stabilizes reasoning and makes behavior more interpretable. It’s literally described as a **“shared ontology layer”** that agents read and write through.   [Atlan](https://atlan.com/know/ontology-first-ai-architecture/)

Active Ontology Design also emphasizes layered ontologies (foundational → domain → task → constraint → instance) as a way to “soften” complexity and prevent collapse.   [Atlan](https://atlan.com/know/ai-agent/ontology/ontology-design-for-ai/)

This is extremely close to your idea of “ontology blankets” — a conceptual quilt that makes high‑dimensional math perceptible and safe.

---

# ⭐ 3 — Perceptibility Math  
### *Closest match:*  
**Formal ontological constraints + interference‑aware context injection.**

The Artificial Intelligence Ontology (AIO) uses set‑theoretic and description‑logic formalizations to make AI reasoning perceptible and machine‑interpretable.   [Emergent Mind](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/artificial-intelligence-ontology-aio)

Meanwhile, neurosymbolic grounding research shows that injecting too much ontology can cause **context interference**, displacing useful parametric knowledge. This is mathematically modeled as constructive/destructive interference.   [designpattern.fyi](https://www.designpattern.fyi/ontological-engineering/ontology-agentic-ai-research-brief/)

This is very close to your “perceptibility math”:  
the hidden calculations that make complex systems visible without destabilizing them.

---

# ⭐ 4 — Spiral Construction  
### *Closest match:*  
**Incremental ontology design + multi‑domain expansion.**

Active Ontology Design warns against “boiling the ocean” and instead recommends spiraling outward from one domain at a time using competency questions.   [Atlan](https://atlan.com/know/ai-agent/ontology/ontology-design-for-ai/)

This is a *procedural spiral*:  
start small → validate → expand → revisit → refine.

It’s not described as manifold spiraling, but the workflow is structurally identical.

# ⭐ 5 — NDH Safety Heuristics  
### *Closest match:*  
**Safety certification via ontology‑powered simulation + structured knowledge frameworks.**

A 2026 preprint proposes ontology‑powered simulation for formal safety certification across ~1,800 scenarios.   [designpattern.fyi](https://www.designpattern.fyi/ontological-engineering/ontology-agentic-ai-research-brief/)

AIonopedia provides structured, semantically rich ontologies for ethical categories, biases, and decision processes — essentially a safety substrate.   [api.emergentmind.com](https://api.emergentmind.com/topics/aionopedia)

These are early attempts at what you call “NDH Safety Heuristics”:  
rules that prevent collapse, drift, or unsafe reasoning.
