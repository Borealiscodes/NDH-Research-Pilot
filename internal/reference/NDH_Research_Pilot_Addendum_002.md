## **1. Updated Role Definition**

**NDH‑Research‑Pilot** is confirmed as the **mid‑altitude research platform** of the NDH ecosystem.  
It provides a safe, non‑runtime environment for:

- soft‑manifold cognition (SCI‑80)  
- holonomy geometry exploration  
- operator behavior testing  
- epistemic dual‑lane methodology  
- serenity‑basin stability modeling  
- ontology fusion prototypes  
- tensor sorting and orbital sweep mechanics  

Research‑Pilot is **not** a trauma‑geometry layer, **not** a high‑dimensional math quarantine layer, and **not** a runtime environment.

This addendum is paired with **NDH‑Simulation‑Suite Addendum‑001**, which clarifies Simulation‑Suite’s role as the high‑dimensional mathematical quarantine layer.

---

## **2. Relationship to Other NDH Subsystems**

- **NDH‑Simulation‑Suite**  
  High‑dimensional math quarantine.  
  Research‑Pilot sits *above* Simulation‑Suite and uses its mathematical artifacts.

- **NDH‑TIDS**  
  Comparative meta‑architecture.  
  Research‑Pilot is *not* part of TIDS; it provides research outputs that TIDS may evaluate.

- **TISD**  
  Trauma‑informed subsystem.  
  Research‑Pilot does *not* host trauma geometry or trauma‑safe operators.

- **NDH‑PLATFORMS**  
  Runtime layer.  
  Research‑Pilot is pre‑runtime and does *not* test haptics or interaction geometry.

---

## **3. Clarification: Promotion Pipeline**

Research‑Pilot is the **A4 → A6 promotion altitude**, supporting:

- **A4 — Exploration**  
- **A5 — Stabilization**  
- **A6 — Governed Research**

This function emerged after the original Simulation‑Suite Sandbox Charter and is now formally recognized.

---

## **4. Charter Context**

The earlier **Simulation‑Suite Sandbox Charter (v1.0)** predates the emergence of NDH‑Research‑Pilot.  
This addendum clarifies that:

- Research‑Pilot did **not** replace the sandbox  
- Research‑Pilot did **not** inherit sandbox governance  
- Research‑Pilot is a **distinct altitude** created to support research and epistemic methodology  
- Simulation‑Suite remains the **high‑dimensional quarantine layer**

This corrects the historical assumption that Simulation‑Suite was the sole validation environment.

---

## **5. Current Status**

NDH‑Research‑Pilot is:

- stable  
- non‑runtime  
- research‑focused  
- mid‑altitude  
- epistemically aligned  
- correct for operator, holonomy, and soft‑manifold testing  

It remains essential for NDH’s cognitive, geometric, and methodological development.
