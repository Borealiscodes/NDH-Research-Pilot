## **3. Holonomy Cycle (Δ → Φ → Ω → Λ → Σ)**

### **[Δ] Legal Salience**  
Identifies what matters legally: operative facts, obligations, violations, jurisdictional triggers.

### **[Φ] Phase Alignment**  
Aligns treaty text, travaux préparatoires, precedent, evidence, and context.

### **[Ω] Holonomy Closure**  
Ensures interpretive loops close consistently across time, tribunals, and jurisdictions.

### **[Λ] Density Regulation**  
Calibrates harm, gravity, severity, and proportionality.

### **[Σ] Synthesis**  
Produces the final legal interpretation or judgment.
