## ⭐ **2 — Primer Structure Template**

This defines the **canonical sections** for any NDH primer.

```
SECTION 1 — Overview
  Provide a high-level description of the conceptual domain, epistemic stance,
  and altitude boundaries. Avoid activation, governance adjacency, or sealed-layer
  references.

SECTION 2 — Core Concepts
  Define the essential terms, primitives, and conceptual anchors required for
  understanding the domain.

SECTION 3 — Structural Grammar
  Provide the structural rules, relationships, and constraints governing the
  conceptual domain. Avoid NDH geometry unless explicitly permitted.

SECTION 4 — Domain Interactions
  Describe how the domain interacts with adjacent domains, epistemic surfaces,
  or modeling layers. Maintain trauma-informed boundaries.

SECTION 5 — Safety Envelope
  Define the non-activation constraints, trauma-informed protections, and
  altitude-safe conditions.

SECTION 6 — Primer Summary
  Provide a concise summary of the primer’s conceptual scope and intended use.
```
