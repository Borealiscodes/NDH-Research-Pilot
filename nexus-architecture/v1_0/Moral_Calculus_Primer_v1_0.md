# ⭐ 1 — What Moral Calculus *Is*  
Moral calculus is the **evaluation grammar** for harm‑phase trajectories.  
It is not:

- ethics  
- governance  
- jurisprudence  
- constitutional logic  
- sealed‑layer reasoning  

Instead, it is:

> **A structural method for evaluating how harm behaves across time, topology, and phase‑space.**

It operates on:

- harm‑phase objects  
- harm‑phase transitions  
- harm‑phase rates  
- harm‑phase surfaces  
- harm‑phase trajectories  

It does **not** interpret meaning.  
It evaluates **structure**.

# ⭐ 2 — The Objects of Moral Calculus  
Moral calculus evaluates the eight harm‑phase objects:

```
L → A → E → D → T → C → X → R
```

Each object has:

- **state geometry**  
- **rate signature**  
- **transition rules**  
- **collapse thresholds**  
- **residual behavior**  

### Example (structural only):

- **L (Latent)** — low activation, high potential  
- **A (Activation)** — rising rate, unstable boundary  
- **E (Escalation)** — acceleration, metastasis risk  
- **D (Damage)** — structural harm, topology deformation  
- **T (Tension)** — load-bearing stress, collapse risk  
- **C (Collapse)** — topology failure, dissipation onset  
- **X (Residual)** — long-tail harm, slow decay  
- **R (Recovery)** — stabilization, return-path geometry  

These are the **inputs** to moral calculus.

# ⭐ 4 — The Rates of Moral Calculus  
Rates are the core of moral calculus.

They are the **derivatives** of harm‑phase behavior:

\[
\dot{A},\ \dot{E},\ \dot{D},\ \dot{T},\ \dot{C},\ \dot{X},\ \dot{R}
\]

Interpretation (structural only):

- **\(\dot{A}^{+}\)** — activation rising  
- **\(\dot{E}^{+}\)** — escalation accelerating  
- **\(\dot{D}^{+}\)** — damage increasing  
- **\(\dot{T}^{+}\)** — tension accumulating  
- **\(\dot{C}^{0}\)** — collapse stable or neutral  
- **\(\dot{X}^{\downarrow}\)** — residual decreasing  
- **\(\dot{R}^{\downarrow}\)** — recovery stabilizing  

Moral calculus evaluates:

- rate magnitude  
- rate direction  
- rate stability  
- rate volatility  

This is the **quantitative grammar**.
