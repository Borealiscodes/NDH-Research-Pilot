# ⭐ **2 — Core Dynamic Modes**

Each begins with a Guided Link.

### **Acceleration**  
Harm increases in intensity or frequency.

### **Deceleration**  
Harm slows but does not resolve.

### **Oscillation**  
Harm cycles between institutions or domains.

### **Propagation**  
Harm spreads across jurisdictions.

### **Metastasis**  
Harm transforms into a new form or domain.

### **Dissipation**  
Harm reduces but leaves structural residue.

### **Stabilization**  
Harm becomes constant, predictable, and chronic.

### **Collapse**  
Systems fail entirely, producing cascading harm.
