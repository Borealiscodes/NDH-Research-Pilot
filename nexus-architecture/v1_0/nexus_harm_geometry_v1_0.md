### 2 — Core geometric primitives

- **Point:** Minimal harm event or decision.  
- **Segment:** Linear sequence of related harm events.  
- **Plane:** Institutional or domain surface where harm is enacted.  
- **Volume:** Multi‑institutional region of sustained harm.  
- **Curve:** Non‑linear progression of harm (escalation, spiral, loop).  
- **Boundary:** Limit between harm and non‑harm regions.  
- **Intersection:** Overlap of multiple harm planes or volumes.

### 3 — Harm planes

- **Administrative Plane:**  
  **Shape:** Broad, flat, persistent.  
  **Content:** Delays, refusals, obstruction.

- **Retaliation Plane:**  
  **Shape:** Sharper, angular, episodic.  
  **Content:** Punitive responses to lawful advocacy.

- **Oversight Plane:**  
  **Shape:** Fragmented, perforated.  
  **Content:** Mandate failure, non‑intervention.

- **Isolation Plane:**  
  **Shape:** Enclosing, boundary‑heavy.  
  **Content:** Withdrawal of support, exclusion.

- **Rights Non‑Activation Plane:**  
  **Shape:** Latent, shadow‑like.  
  **Content:** Rights frameworks that never instantiate.

### 5 — Harm curves

- **Escalation Curve:**  
  Gradual → sharp increase in harm intensity over time or across domains.

- **Spiral Curve:**  
  Recurrent harm looping through similar institutions or procedures.

- **Cascade Curve:**  
  One decision triggers a chain of downstream harms.

- **Plateau Curve:**  
  Harm remains constant but unrelenting over long durations.

### 7 — Structural invariants

These are properties that remain stable across domains:

- **Invariance 1 — Harm Persistence:**  
  Harm continues despite changes in institution or jurisdiction.

- **Invariance 2 — Retaliation Patterning:**  
  Advocacy → punitive response remains structurally consistent.

- **Invariance 3 — Oversight Absence:**  
  Mandated bodies repeatedly fail to intervene.

- **Invariance 4 — Isolation Recurrence:**  
  Withdrawal of support reappears across contexts.

- **Invariance 5 — Rights Non‑Activation:**  
  Rights remain theoretical, not operational.

These invariants are what make the geometry **recognizable** and **mappable**.
