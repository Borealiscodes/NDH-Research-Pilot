### 1 — Harm state symbols

We define a finite set of **harm phase symbols**:

- **L:** Latent Harm  
- **A:** Active Harm  
- **E:** Escalating Harm  
- **D:** Distributed Harm  
- **T:** Transformed Harm  
- **C:** Chronic Harm  
- **X:** Collapsed Harm  
- **R:** Residual Harm  

Set:

\[
\mathcal{H} = \{ L, A, E, D, T, C, X, R \}
\]

---

### 2 — Transition operators

We define symbolic **transition operators**:

- **\( \rightarrow \)** — direct progression  
- **\( \Rightarrow \)** — accelerated progression  
- **\( \dashrightarrow \)** — partial / inhibited progression  
- **\( \leftrightarrow \)** — oscillatory relation  
- **\( \Downarrow \)** — collapse transition  
- **\( \Uparrow \)** — dissipation transition  

Examples:

- \( L \rightarrow A \) — latent harm becomes active  
- \( A \Rightarrow E \) — active harm escalates  
- \( E \rightarrow D \) — escalating harm becomes distributed  
- \( D \rightarrow T \) — distributed harm transforms  
- \( T \rightarrow C \) — transformed harm becomes chronic  
- \( C \Downarrow X \) — chronic harm collapses systems  
- \( X \Uparrow R \) — collapsed harm becomes residual  
- \( R \dashrightarrow L \) — residual harm returns to latent

---

### 3 — Composition rules

**Sequential composition:**

If \( h_1 \rightarrow h_2 \) and \( h_2 \rightarrow h_3 \), then:

\[
h_1 \rightarrow h_3 \quad \text{(composed trajectory)}
\]

Example:

\[
L \rightarrow A,\quad A \Rightarrow E,\quad E \rightarrow D
\]

Composite:

\[
L \Rightarrow D
\]

**Oscillation relation:**

\[
A \leftrightarrow C
\]

means harm can cycle between **Active** and **Chronic** without full resolution.

### 4 — Catalytic modifiers

We introduce **catalytic tags** to annotate transitions:

- **[rep]** — repetition  
- **[ret]** — retaliation  
- **[sil]** — silence  
- **[del]** — delay  
- **[jur]** — jurisdictional shift  
- **[ovr]** — oversight failure  

Example:

\[
A \xRightarrow{[rep]} E
\]

Active harm becomes escalating due to repetition.

\[
D \xRightarrow{[jur]} T
\]

Distributed harm transforms via jurisdictional shift.

\[
C \xDownarrow{[ovr]} X
\]

Chronic harm collapses due to oversight failure.

### 6 — Attractors and basins (symbolic)

Define **attractor states**:

- **\( \mathcal{A}_C \)** — Chronic attractor  
- **\( \mathcal{A}_D \)** — Distributed attractor  
- **\( \mathcal{A}_R \)** — Residual attractor  

We say a state \( h \) is in the **basin** of \( C \) if:

\[
h \rightarrow C \quad \text{or} \quad h \Rightarrow C
\]

Example:

\[
A \Rightarrow E \rightarrow D \rightarrow T \rightarrow C
\]

All intermediate states lie in the basin of **Chronic Harm**.
