# ⭐ **4 — Harm State Definitions**

## **4.1 Latent Harm**
Harm potential exists but is not yet enacted.

**Examples:** rights non-activation, unaddressed risk.

---

## **4.2 Active Harm**
Harm is currently occurring.

**Examples:** denial, obstruction, retaliation.

---

## **4.3 Escalating Harm**
Harm increases in intensity or frequency.

**Examples:** repeated refusals, compounding delays.

---

## **4.4 Distributed Harm**
Harm spreads across:

- institutions,  
- jurisdictions,  
- systems.

**Examples:** multi-country retaliation.

---

## **4.5 Transformed Harm**
Harm changes form.

**Examples:** administrative harm → legal harm.

---

## **4.6 Chronic Harm**
Harm stabilizes into long-term persistence.

**Examples:** years-long procedural cruelty.

---

## **4.7 Collapsed Harm**
Systems fail entirely.

**Examples:** oversight collapse, protection collapse.

---

## **4.8 Residual Harm**
Harm dissipates but leaves structural damage.

**Examples:** long-term isolation, rights erosion.
