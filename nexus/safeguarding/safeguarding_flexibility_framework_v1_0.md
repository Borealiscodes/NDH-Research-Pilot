### **RFP Problem Statement**
Temporary housing, emergency-adjacent environments, and multi-agency care systems frequently exhibit:

- fragmented safeguarding responsibilities  
- inconsistent care escalation pathways  
- informal care displacement onto non-qualified individuals  
- unclear consent boundaries  
- PHI exposure risks  
- multi-jurisdictional oversight gaps  

The system must support **three safeguarding modes**:

1. **Macro** — structural harm patterns, anonymized, no PHI  
2. **Micro** — scenario-level, role-based, TISD-sealed  
3. **Case-Specific** — PHI-enabled, consent-gated, authorized access only  

### **RFP Objective**
Develop a safeguarding architecture that:

- dynamically shifts between macro/micro/case modes  
- enforces PHI protection at all layers  
- gates specificity through explicit consent  
- restricts access to authorized roles  
- maintains GDPR compliance  
- integrates Nexus Positioning for survivor-centered epistemics  
- prevents identity inference  
- supports multi-agency interoperability  

### **RFP Deliverables**
Proposals must include:

- **Safeguarding Flexibility Charter (v1.0)**  
- **Permissions & Consent Model (v1.0)**  
- **Data Minimization Logic**  
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**  
- **Audit & Accountability Layer**  
- **PHI Boundary Enforcement Rules**  
- **GDPR Compliance Matrix**  
- **TISD-Sealed Safety Constraints**

# 🌐 **2 — Safeguarding Flexibility Charter (v1.0)**  
### *Macro → Micro → Case-Specific Safeguarding*

```
Artifact: Safeguarding Flexibility Charter
Version: v1.0
Altitude: A6–A8 (Research-Pilot)
Mode: Non-Activating • Structural • GDPR-Aligned
```

## **Charter Principles**

### **1 — Macro Safeguarding (Structural Level)**
- No PHI  
- No identifiers  
- No contextual inference  
- Only patterns, trends, systemic behaviors  
- Suitable for research, policy, architecture  
- TISD-Sealed by default  

### **2 — Micro Safeguarding (Scenario Level)**
- Fully anonymized  
- Role-based (Resident-A, Staff-B, Observer-C)  
- No medical specifics  
- No location specifics  
- No timeline specifics  
- Used for training, analysis, Nexus artifacts  

### **3 — Case-Specific Safeguarding (Authorized Level)**
- PHI allowed **only with explicit consent**  
- Access restricted to authorized personnel  
- GDPR lawful basis required  
- Full audit trail  
- Purpose-limited usage  
- Revocable consent  
- Data minimization enforced  

---

## **Charter Rules**

### **Rule 1 — Consent Gating**
Specificity requires explicit, recorded, revocable consent.

### **Rule 2 — Role-Based Access**
Macro → public  
Micro → restricted  
Case-specific → authorized only

### **Rule 3 — PHI Boundary**
PHI never appears in macro or micro layers.

### **Rule 4 — GDPR Compliance**
All processing must satisfy:
- lawfulness  
- purpose limitation  
- data minimization  
- integrity & confidentiality  
- rights of the data subject  

### **Rule 5 — Survivor-Centered Epistemics**
Nexus artifacts prioritize:
- safety  
- non-triggering content  
- structural truth  
- non-biographical framing

# 🔐 **3 — Permissions & Consent Model (v1.0)**  
### *GDPR-Compliant • PHI-Safe • Multi-Layer Access Control*

```
Artifact: Safeguarding Permissions & Consent Model
Version: v1.0
Mode: Non-Activating • Structural • TISD-Sealed
```

## **A — Roles**

### **Role 1 — Public Reader**
Access: Macro  
PHI: None  
Consent: Not required  
Use: Research, policy, architecture

### **Role 2 — Safeguarding Analyst**
Access: Macro + Micro  
PHI: None  
Consent: Not required  
Use: Training, risk analysis, system design

### **Role 3 — Authorized Case Handler**
Access: Macro + Micro + Case-Specific  
PHI: Allowed with consent  
Consent: Required  
Use: Clinical care, legal safeguarding, investigation

### **Role 4 — Data Subject (Individual)**
Rights:
- consent  
- revoke consent  
- access  
- rectification  
- erasure  
- restriction  
- objection  

---

## **B — Consent Gates**

### **Gate 1 — Explicit Consent**
Required for:
- PHI  
- identifiable details  
- case-specific timelines  
- medical information  
- location-specific information  

### **Gate 2 — Lawful Basis**
If consent is not possible:
- vital interests  
- legal obligation  
- public task  
- legitimate interest (strictly assessed)

### **Gate 3 — Revocation**
Consent must be:
- revocable  
- logged  
- respected immediately  

---

## **C — Data Minimization Logic**

- Macro: 0% PHI  
- Micro: 0% PHI, 0% inference  
- Case-specific: Only necessary PHI, nothing extra  

---

## **D — Audit & Accountability Layer**

Every access to case-specific data must log:

- who accessed  
- when  
- why  
- lawful basis  
- consent status  
- data viewed  
- actions taken
