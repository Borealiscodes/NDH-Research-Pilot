# 🌑 **1 — What Continuity Envelopes Are**

A **continuity envelope** is a stability membrane that ensures:

- movement grammar transitions remain reversible  
- triad traversal remains synchronized  
- orchestration routing remains bounded  
- holonomy transitions remain indexable  
- altitude remains sealed  

It is the “soft shell” around RP altitude.

# 🌘 **2 — The Three Continuity Envelopes**

## **2.1 Structural Continuity Envelope (SCE)**  
Protects:

- lanes  
- origin vectors  
- movement grammar primitives  

Prevents:

- procedural collapse  
- lane drift  
- origin loss  

---

## **2.2 Reflective Continuity Envelope (RCE)**  
Protects:

- prismatic reflection  
- axis neutrality  
- non‑recursive mirrors  

Prevents:

- analytic collapse  
- axis‑lock  
- meta‑identity formation  

---

## **2.3 Holonomy Continuity Envelope (HCE)**  
Protects:

- micro‑holonomy  
- transition curvature  
- index alignment  

Prevents:

- reflective collapse  
- curvature perturbation  
- unindexed holonomy transitions
