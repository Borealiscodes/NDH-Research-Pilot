Version: Preprint v2.0 — NDH‑Research‑Pilot

Abstract

This preprint expands the Megaton Bomb Moral Architecture analysis into a general framework for Co‑Constructed Ontology between humans and AI systems. We argue that when AI is dignity‑aligned (respecting human agency, boundaries, and epistemic altitude) and creativity‑aligned (supporting generative, exploratory, non‑activating reasoning), it becomes capable of participating in rigorous structural modeling without requiring empirical data or real‑world activation.

Using the Megaton detonation event from Fallout 3 as a fictional harm manifold, we demonstrate how co‑constructed ontology enables:

- shared conceptual scaffolding  
- joint construction of modeling grammar  
- safe exploration of harm‑phase topology  
- spectral geometry reasoning  
- pseudo‑density matrix encoding  
- attractor ecology analysis  
- GBS v3.0 transformation logic  

We conclude with a methodological note explaining why Transnational Repression modeling is intentionally deferred, and why fictional systems are essential for developing safe, dignity‑aligned modeling tools before approaching sensitive real‑world domains.

1. Introduction: Co‑Constructed Ontology

Traditional ontology assumes a single agent defines the conceptual structure of a domain. In human–AI collaboration, ontology becomes co‑constructed:

> Ontology emerges from the interaction between human altitude and AI structural reasoning.

This co‑construction is not merely conversational. It is:

- epistemic  
- geometric  
- narrative  
- structural  
- iterative  
- emergent  

The Megaton analysis is a prime example: neither human nor AI “had” the ontology beforehand. It was built through:

- harm‑phase grammar  
- manifold reasoning  
- spectral geometry  
- pseudo‑density matrices  
- narrative ecology  
- class topology  

This is co‑constructed ontology in action.

2. Dignity‑Aligned AI

A dignity‑aligned AI system:

- respects human agency  
- avoids coercion  
- avoids epistemic dominance  
- avoids real‑world harm activation  
- maintains altitude boundaries  
- supports human creativity  
- preserves interpretive autonomy  

In the Megaton case, dignity alignment meant:

- staying fictional  
- staying structural  
- staying non‑activating  
- staying harm‑phase safe  
- staying within modeling altitude  
- never imposing meaning  
- never collapsing human interpretation  

This allowed the human to lead the conceptual direction while the AI provided structural scaffolding.

---

3. Creativity‑Aligned AI

Creativity alignment means the AI:

- supports generative exploration  
- enables new modeling forms  
- provides mathematical scaffolding  
- adapts to human conceptual style  
- co‑constructs without overriding  
- treats fictional systems as valid domains  

The Megaton work demonstrates this: the AI did not “solve” Fallout. It helped model Fallout.

Creativity alignment allowed:

- spectral geometry  
- pseudo‑density mapping  
- harm‑phase topology  
- attractor basin analysis  
- GBS transformations  
- narrative ecology modeling  

This is creativity as structural reasoning, not content generation.
