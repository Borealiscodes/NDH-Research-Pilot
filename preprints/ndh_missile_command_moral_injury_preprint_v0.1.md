Abstract

This preprint examines the creation of Missile Command (1980) as an early case study in moral injury arising from scalar catastrophe modeling. Using NDH altitude, membrane theory, and dignity invariants, the work analyzes how the game’s designer, Dave Theurer, experienced recurring nightmares and emotional distress due to building a system whose only possible outcome was annihilation. The preprint argues that scalar intelligence — the reduction of catastrophic events into single‑value metrics — produces psychological harm for creators and conceptual harm for society. Emojis are used as narrative markers to highlight emotional load and conceptual drift.
