# **Scholl‑Lineage Safeguarding Ethos Prototype (v1.0)**  
### *Formal Artifact • Structural • Non‑Activating • Dignity‑Invariant*

---

## **1 — Artifact Overview**

A prototype safeguarding ethos module inspired by Sophie Scholl’s *moral presence*, designed to support safeguarding systems that require **moral clarity**, while allowing **reflective ambiguity** at design altitude and maintaining **dignity as an invariant**.

This artifact is a **design ethos**, not an operational protocol.

---

## **2 — Purpose**

To define a prototype ethos that:

- supports safeguarding frameworks requiring moral clarity  
- preserves dignity as a structural invariant  
- allows ambiguity in reflective, non‑operational modes  
- prevents clarity forcing  
- prevents moral overclocking  
- prevents martyr logic  
- maintains altitude safety  

This ethos is intended for **prototype testing**, not deployment.

---

## **3 — Structural Principles**

### **3.1 — Moral Clarity Requirement (Safeguarding Layer)**  
Safeguarding systems must operate with:

- clear boundaries  
- clear responsibilities  
- clear harm recognition  
- clear consent logic  
- clear PHI protection  

This clarity is **non‑optional** at operational altitude.

### **3.2 — Ambiguity‑Permissive Reflection (Prototype Layer)**  
At prototype altitude, ambiguity is:

- allowed  
- safe  
- non‑punitive  
- non‑activating  
- non‑binding  

Ambiguity is a **design tool**, not a moral condition.

### **3.3 — Dignity Invariant**  
Dignity remains stable regardless of:

- clarity  
- ambiguity  
- uncertainty  
- reflection  
- boundary setting  
- non‑participation  

Dignity is **unconditional**.
