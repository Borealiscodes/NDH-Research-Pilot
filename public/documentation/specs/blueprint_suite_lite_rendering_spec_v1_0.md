# 🌿 **Blueprint Suite Lite — Rendering Spec (v1.0)**  
### *Non‑Activating • Public‑Safe • Constellation‑Adjacent Without Governance*

---

## **1. Purpose**
Provide a **stable, gentle rendering specification** for emblem creation within NDH‑Research‑Pilot.  
This suite governs **visual coherence only**, not system routing, operator behavior, holonomy, or constellation governance.

It ensures:

- consistent geometry  
- stable metaphors  
- non‑activating visual language  
- safe constellation adjacency  
- clear separation from NDH‑Constellation governance layers  

---

## **2. Rendering Principles**

### **2.1 Transparency First**
All emblem renderings must use a **transparent background** to ensure:

- accessibility  
- portability  
- non‑hierarchical presentation  
- compatibility with public profiles  

Transparency acts as the “open meadow” layer.

---

### **2.2 Rainbow Enso Crown**
The **rainbow enso 🌈** is the crown element.

Rendering rules:

- stroke weight: **soft**  
- gradient: **continuous**, no sharp transitions  
- geometry: **closed loop**, symbolizing gentle coherence  
- placement: **top-center**, never implying authority  

The enso is a **symbol of presence**, not governance.

---

### **2.3 Meadow Layer**
The meadow is the **calm substrate**.

Rendering rules:

- color palette: soft greens, muted golds, gentle blues  
- texture: watercolor or soft‑grain  
- density: low, to avoid expressive jets  
- Quantum Forest undergrowth: subtle, woven into the meadow base  

The meadow expresses **gentle cognition** and **soft‑manifold behavior**.

---

### **2.4 Hexagon Constellation System**
Hexagons represent **cooperative geometry**.

Rendering rules:

- hexagon count: 3–12  
- adjacency: no more than 2 layers deep  
- opacity: 40–70%  
- placement: distributed, not hierarchical  
- avoid: operator‑like routing lines  

Hexagons must **not** resemble NDH‑Constellation subsystem maps.

---

### **2.5 Celestial Triad (Sun, Moon, Earth)**
The celestial bodies express **non‑linear evolution** and **spiral geometry**.

Rendering rules:

- size: small icons  
- placement: at stable hexagon nodes  
- style: soft, symbolic, non‑literal  
- motion cues: none (static only)  

No orbital paths, no holonomy arcs.

---

### **2.6 Spiral Geometry (Optional)**
If included:

- spirals must be **subtle**  
- opacity: < 30%  
- curvature: gentle, non‑directional  
- placement: background layer only  

Spirals express **returning-with-change**, not routing.

---

## **3. Stability Constraints**

### **3.1 Non‑Activation Rule**
The emblem must **not**:

- imply operator sequencing  
- resemble holonomy routing  
- depict constellation subsystem adjacency  
- include directional arrows  
- include curvature maps  
- include tensor glyphs  

This keeps the emblem strictly within **RP altitude**.

---

### **3.2 Non‑Governance Rule**
The emblem must **not**:

- sit atop a shield  
- include framing bars  
- include hierarchical geometry  
- include crown-like structures beyond the enso  

The enso is symbolic, not authoritative.

---

### **3.3 Non‑Contamination Rule**
The emblem must not borrow:

- NDH‑Constellation subsystem geometry  
- NDH‑Core invariants  
- Zen‑Bridge resonance glyphs  
- Trauma‑Informed Systems Design icons  

It remains a **Research‑Pilot artifact only**.

---

## **4. Rendering Pipeline**

### **4.1 Layer Order**
1. Transparent background  
2. Meadow + Quantum Forest undergrowth  
3. Hexagon constellation  
4. Celestial triad  
5. Spiral geometry (optional)  
6. Rainbow enso crown  

---

### **4.2 Color Discipline**
Use a **constellation‑safe palette**:

- soft greens  
- muted gold  
- gentle blues  
- pastel rainbow  
- low‑saturation earth tones  

Avoid:

- neon  
- high‑contrast jets  
- operator‑adjacent blues  

---

### **4.3 Geometry Discipline**
- hexagons only  
- circles only  
- spirals only  
- no triangles  
- no arrows  
- no routing lines  

This prevents accidental subsystem adjacency.

---

## **5. Recommended Emblem Architecture**
The suite is optimized for:

- **Geometric Constellation Emblem**  
- **Painterly Meadow Emblem**  

It can support:

- **Minimal Symbolic Emblem**  
…but minimalism rarely needs a suite.
