# 🌈 **Constellation‑Safe Color Palette (v1.0)**  
### *NDH‑Research‑Pilot • Non‑Activating • Chromatic Stability Specification*

---

## **1. Purpose**
Define a **stable, constellation‑safe color palette** for emblem creation within NDH‑Research‑Pilot altitude.  
The palette must:

- support the Emblem Design Brief  
- maintain accessibility  
- avoid subsystem adjacency  
- avoid governance posture  
- express gentle cognitive geometry  
- preserve the metaphors (enso, meadow, hexagons, celestial triad, Quantum Forest)  
- remain safe for public‑facing identity  

This palette is the chromatic foundation for the **Rendering Pipeline Diagram**.

---

# **2. Chromatic Principles**

### **2.1 Softness First**
Colors must be:

- low‑saturation  
- non‑neon  
- non‑metallic  
- non‑directional  

This prevents expressive jets and operator adjacency.

### **2.2 Constellation‑Safe Geometry Colors**
Hexagon geometry must use:

- muted blues  
- soft greens  
- gentle golds  

These colors express cooperative cognition without resembling subsystem maps.

### **2.3 Meadow & Forest Discipline**
Meadow and Quantum Forest undergrowth must use:

- earth‑rooted greens  
- moss tones  
- muted soil browns  
- soft sky blues  

These colors maintain ecological gentleness.

### **2.4 Rainbow Enso Gradient**
The enso uses a **pastel rainbow**, not a saturated one.

Gradient order:

1. soft red  
2. peach  
3. gold  
4. mint  
5. sky blue  
6. lavender  

This expresses plurality without hierarchy.

### **2.5 Celestial Triad Colors**
Sun, moon, and earth must use:

- sun: warm gold  
- moon: silver‑grey  
- earth: muted blue‑green  

These colors express non‑linear evolution without implying orbital routing.

# **4. Stability Constraints**

### **4.1 No High‑Contrast Jets**
Avoid:

- neon  
- saturated primaries  
- metallic gradients  
- operator‑adjacent blues  

### **4.2 No Governance Colors**
Avoid:

- deep navy  
- hard black  
- metallic gold  
- crimson hierarchy tones  

### **4.3 No Routing Colors**
Avoid:

- arrow‑adjacent reds  
- holonomy‑adjacent purples  
- tensor‑adjacent blues  

---

# **5. Palette Usage Rules**

### **5.1 Geometry**
Hexagons must use:

- constellation blue  
- tessellation green  
- soft gold  

### **5.2 Substrate**
Meadow must use:

- soft meadow green  
- moss green  
- sky mist blue  

### **5.3 Crown**

- pastel gradient only  

### **5.4 Celestial Triad**

- sun gold  
- moon silver  
- earth blue‑green

- moss green
