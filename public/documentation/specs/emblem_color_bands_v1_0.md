### *NDH‑Research‑Pilot • Non‑Activating • ASCII‑Safe Chromatic Bands*

## **1. Purpose**
Provide **linear, ASCII‑safe, non‑activating color bands** representing the full emblem palette.  
These bands:

- stabilize chromatic interpretation  
- provide GitHub‑compatible visual references  
- bridge conceptual palette → machine‑readable palette  
- maintain strict RP altitude  
- avoid subsystem adjacency, routing, or governance cues  

They do **not** represent literal colors — they are symbolic tiles.
