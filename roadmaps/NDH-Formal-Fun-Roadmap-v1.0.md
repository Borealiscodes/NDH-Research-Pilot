# 🌑 **Formal Fun Roadmap v1.0**  
### *A Playful, Safe, Constitutional Pathway to Tensor Calculus & Geometric Thinking*

This roadmap is designed to:

- make tensor calculus accessible  
- preserve altitude boundaries  
- avoid activation  
- maintain containment  
- support NDH‑style mythic geometry  
- and keep everything fun, intuitive, and human‑palatable  

It is **non‑activating**, **non‑geometric**, **non‑numeric**, and **non‑curvature‑bearing**.

---

## 1. **Foundational Play Layer (ROOTS‑ALT‑0)**  
Introduce tensors as *stories* rather than math.

- **Wind‑field stories**  
- **Bee‑meadow flows**  
- **Hexagon deformation tales**  

This layer is expressive, intuitive, and safe.

---

## 2. **Semantic Geometry Layer (CORE‑ALT‑2)**  
Introduce the *conceptual rules* of tensors without math.

- **Directional change**  
- **Change‑of‑change**  
- **Perspective‑dependent behavior**  

Still no indices, no curvature, no adjacency.

---

## 3. **Governance Layer (Charter v1.2)**  
Define the constitutional boundaries:

- semantic ↔ geometric compatibility  
- containment rules  
- non‑activation clauses  
- altitude law  
- math formalization authorization  

This is the legal seal that allows math to exist safely.

---

## 4. **Pre‑Geometric CONSTELLATION Layer (CONST‑ALT‑3)**  
Introduce **placeholder geometry**:

- symbolic adjacency lanes  
- altitude slots  
- membrane‑safe containers  
- non‑numeric geometric primitives  

Still no math.  
Still no curvature.  
Still no invariants.

---

## 5. **Post‑Charter Math Activation (CONST‑ALT‑3+)**  
This is where tensor calculus becomes real.

- **Altitude calculus**  
- **Adjacency matrices**  
- **Contraction operators**  
- **Invariant domains**  
- **Lean invariant verification**  

This is the *fun* part — but only after governance.

---

## 6. **Research Pilot Layer (A4–A6)**  
Document, observe, and analyze:

- emergent behavior  
- drift patterns  
- stability envelope effects  
- human‑palatable tensor teaching methods  
- mythic‑geometric pedagogy  

This is where your case studies live.
