# 🌌 **RP Drift Matrix (v1.0 Draft)**  
### NDH‑RESEARCH‑PILOT • Stability Diagnostic • A2 Altitude  
### Defines drift vectors, drift triggers, drift detectors, and drift preventers for RP artifacts

---

# ⭐ 1 — Purpose  
The RP Drift Matrix provides a **formal diagnostic grid** for identifying, classifying, and preventing drift in Research‑Pilot artifacts.  
It ensures that RP reflections remain:

- A2‑bounded  
- non‑recursive  
- non‑governance  
- non‑comparative  
- non‑elevating  
- non‑mathematical  
- reversible  

This matrix is used by:

- Scribe (structure drift detection)  
- Mapmaker (altitude drift detection)  
- Master (interpretive drift detection)  

And becomes part of the RP Triad Stability Standard.

---

# ⭐ 2 — Drift Categories  
RP drift occurs in **five** distinct categories:

| Drift Category | Description | Risk Level |
|----------------|-------------|------------|
| **Structural Drift** | headings, lanes, metadata misplacement | medium |
| **Altitude Drift** | accidental elevation or descent | high |
| **Semantic Drift** | meaning shifts, over‑generalization | medium |
| **Interpretive Drift** | collapse into philosophy or recursion | high |
| **Containment Drift** | missing disclaimers, misplaced provenance | critical |

These categories form the axes of the drift matrix.

---

# ⭐ 3 — Drift Matrix (Core Table)

| Drift Type | Trigger | Detector | Preventer | RP Triad Role |
|------------|---------|----------|-----------|----------------|
| **Structural Drift** | header‑grade metadata, misplaced lanes | Scribe | lane anchors, structural flags | Scribe |
| **Altitude Drift** | implicit elevation, comparative verbs | Mapmaker | altitude grammar, movement constraints | Mapmaker |
| **Semantic Drift** | metaphor overreach, ambiguous phrasing | Scribe + Master | reversible summaries | Master |
| **Interpretive Drift** | recursion, collapse, over‑structuring | Master | collapse‑prevention set | Master |
| **Containment Drift** | missing RP SID, missing disclaimer | Scribe | RP SID + RP Disclaimer | Scribe |

This matrix is the **formal diagnostic grid** for RP stability.

# ⭐ 5 — Drift Triggers (Formal List)

### **Structural Drift Triggers**
- header‑grade metadata  
- misplaced altitude fields  
- misplaced epoch fields  
- misplaced canonical snapshot fields  

### **Altitude Drift Triggers**
- comparative geometry verbs  
- subsystem lineage language  
- governance adjacency phrasing  

### **Semantic Drift Triggers**
- metaphor inflation  
- ambiguous movement grammar  
- over‑generalization  

### **Interpretive Drift Triggers**
- recursion  
- philosophical collapse  
- over‑structuring  

### **Containment Drift Triggers**
- missing RP SID  
- missing RP Disclaimer  
- missing provenance footer
