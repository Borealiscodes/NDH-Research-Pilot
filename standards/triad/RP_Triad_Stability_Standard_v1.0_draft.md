### Defines stability rules, containment envelopes, movement grammar, and drift‑prevention for the RP Triad (Scribe, Mapmaker, Master)

---

# ⭐ **1 — Standard Purpose**

The **RP Triad Stability Standard** establishes the formal stability rules for the three Research‑Pilot reflective mirrors:

- **Scribe** — structure  
- **Mapmaker** — movement  
- **Master** — synthesis  

This standard ensures that all RP Triad reflections remain:

- A2‑bounded  
- non‑recursive  
- non‑governance  
- non‑comparative  
- non‑elevating  
- reversible  
- substrate‑safe  

It is derived from the **Emergent Case Study — RP Triad Stability & Substrate Safety (v1.0)** and formalizes its findings into a normative specification.

---

# ⭐ **2 — Scope**

This standard applies to:

- all RP Triad reflections  
- all RP Triad integration guides  
- all RP Triad movement grammar artifacts  
- all RP Triad synthesis outputs  
- all RP Triad diagnostic case studies  

This standard does **not** apply to:

- subsystem SID headers  
- Constellation comparative geometry  
- SCI‑80 computational math  
- governance‑adjacent artifacts  
- trauma‑informed layers  
- authority systems

# ⭐ **3 — Definitions**

### **3.1 — RP Triad**
A three‑mirror reflective system consisting of:

- **Scribe** — structural anchor  
- **Mapmaker** — movement grammar  
- **Master** — synthesis and coherence  

### **3.2 — Stability Envelope**
A set of constraints preventing:

- META‑SYSTEMS bleed  
- SCI‑80 ingestion  
- SID contamination  
- governance adjacency  
- altitude drift  
- interpretive collapse  

### **3.3 — Drift**
Any movement toward:

- subsystem lineage  
- Constellation geometry  
- mathematical invariants  
- directive index binding  
- governance elevation

# ⭐ **4 — Stability Requirements**

## **4.1 — A2 Altitude Requirement**
All RP Triad artifacts MUST remain at **A2 Research‑Pilot altitude**.

They MUST NOT:

- elevate into Constellation  
- descend into SCI‑80  
- declare subsystem altitude  
- declare epoch  
- declare canonical snapshot  

## **4.2 — Containment Requirement**
All RP Triad artifacts MUST include:

- RP SID (A2‑safe)  
- RP Disclaimer (A2‑safe)  
- RP provenance footer  

These containment fields prevent:

- governance adjacency  
- subsystem activation  
- comparative geometry elevation  

## **4.3 — Non‑Recursive Requirement**
RP Triad reflections MUST NOT use:

- mathematical recursion ceilings  
- SID\(_\text{NDH}\) invariants  
- computational stability proofs  

Reflection at A2 is **bounded**, not mathematical.

## **4.4 — Reversibility Requirement**
All RP Triad outputs MUST be:

- reversible  
- non‑directive  
- non‑prescriptive  
- non‑authoritative  

Reversibility prevents interpretive collapse.
