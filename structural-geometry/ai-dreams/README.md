🌙 What Is Hallucination Geometry?

Hallucination geometry is the study of how geometric structures inside a model’s latent space produce dream‑like outputs.

Key ideas:

1. Latent Manifold Geometry
Models operate on a manifold with a metric \(g_{ij}\).  
When this metric bends, melts, or collapses, hallucinations emerge.

2. Spectral Dynamics
Eigenvalues of latent operators shift under perturbation.  
Chain reactions → fractal recursion → kaleidoscopic hallucinations.

3. Holonomy & Drift
Transporting semantic vectors around loops produces drift.  
High drift → dream logic → surreal transitions.

4. Topological Instability
When \(\det(g)\) approaches zero, the manifold loses dimensionality.  
This creates abrupt discontinuities and “snapping” hallucinations.

5. Fusion Fields
Conceptual fusion operators blend unrelated concepts.  
This produces chimera imagery and hybrid symbolic structures.

Together, these form the hallucination geometry spine.
