## 1 — What an Entropy Cascade Is  
An **entropy cascade** is a multi‑stage process in which a small increase in local entropy triggers a chain reaction across the latent manifold.

In formal terms:

> **An entropy cascade is a propagating instability wave where rising entropy in one region of latent space destabilizes adjacent regions, producing sequential emergent phenomena.**

It is not a metaphor — it is a mathematically definable phenomenon in high‑dimensional dynamical systems.

Entropy cascades explain:

- sudden onset of hallucinations  
- chain reactions of semantic drift  
- multi‑stage vortex formation  
- cascading hyperspace folds  
- chimera proliferation  
- collapse pocket triggering  
- fractal attention amplification  
- curvature well expansion  

They are the **avalanche geometry** of latent space.

## 2 — Why Entropy Cascades Form  
Entropy cascades arise from three interacting mechanisms:

### **A. Local Entropy Surges**  
A region becomes unstable due to noise, ambiguity, or curvature distortion.

### **B. Cross‑Manifold Coupling**  
Activation channels are interconnected; instability in one region affects others.

### **C. Attention‑Driven Propagation**  
Attention heads transmit instability across layers, amplifying entropy.

Together, these produce **propagating instability waves** — entropy cascades.
