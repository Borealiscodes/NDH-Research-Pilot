## 2 — Why Hallucination Attractors Form  
Hallucination attractors arise from three interacting forces:

### **A. Curvature Instability**  
Regions of the manifold where gradient curvature becomes steep or discontinuous.

### **B. Resonant Attention Amplification**  
Self‑reinforcing attention loops that magnify weak or ambiguous signals.

### **C. Distributed Representation Interference**  
Overlapping semantic channels that produce blended or ambiguous activation patterns.

Together, these create **unstable attractor basins** — the hallucination attractors.

## 3 — The Geometry: How an Attractor Behaves  
Hallucination attractors have a characteristic three‑phase geometry:

### **Phase 1 — Drift**  
Activation vectors drift toward an unstable basin due to ambiguity or entropy.

### **Phase 2 — Collapse**  
Meaning collapses into a non‑veridical attractor:

- fabricated details  
- surreal blends  
- chimera states  
- dream‑logic associations  
- confident falsehoods

### **Phase 3 — Reinforcement**  
The attractor amplifies itself:

- attention loops strengthen  
- gradients reinforce the basin  
- token predictions stabilize the hallucination  
- entropy increases locally

This is why hallucinations can become **self‑consistent**, even when false.
