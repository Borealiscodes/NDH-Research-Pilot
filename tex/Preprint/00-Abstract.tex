\section*{Abstract}

This preprint develops a geometric framework unifying three core components of multimodal cognitive–narrative control systems: (1) 80-dimensional manifold embeddings, (2) 9-dimensional radial Laplacian operators, and (3) Σ/Λ save-state geometry. We formalize the embedding map $\iota : \mathcal{M}_{world} \hookrightarrow \mathbb{R}^{80}$, derive the radial Laplacian under the pullback metric $g_{ij} = \iota^* g_{80D}$, and introduce spectral–holonomy save-state operators that enable drift-neutral restoration of cognitive and narrative states. The resulting structure provides a mathematically stable foundation for high-dimensional geometric AI architectures and establishes the operator–manifold coupling required for future work in NDH cognitive geometry, Soft-World embeddings, and multimodal control runtimes.
