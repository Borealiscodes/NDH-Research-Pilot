#### 2 — Why a Construction Suite is necessary

- **Machine‑readable dashboards** now exist (spec, panels, visual grammar).  
- **Rendering invariants** (PRECL_R, ABV_R, TPCV_R, TSV_R, LSV_R, CPV_R, MTV_R) are defined in the rendering ecosystem.  
- **Failure modes** such as altitude collapse, membrane breach, geometry drift, palette contamination, provenance fracture, sequencing inversion, apex resonance leakage, and PRECL collapse failure are already identified.  

> “Failure modes now include altitude collapse, membrane breach, geometry drift, palette contamination, provenance fracture, sequencing inversion, apex resonance leakage, PRECL collapse failure.”  

Without a Construction Suite, these constraints and failure modes are **not enforced** across:

- all panels,  
- all visualization types,  
- all machine‑readable schemas,  
- all rendering engines.

The suite becomes the **enforcement mechanism** and **coordination layer**.

---

#### 3 — Scope of the Construction Suite

The TISD Dashboard Construction Suite will:

- **Define machine‑readable schemas** for:  
  - panel metadata,  
  - viz_tokens,  
  - harm‑topology graphs,  
  - burden‑shift lattices,  
  - treaty‑layer overlays,  
  - echo‑pathway flows,  
  - apex vacuum maps.  

- **Bind the rendering pipeline**:  
  - data → PRECL collapse → invariant checks → SVG/PNG/heat‑map render → dashboard panel.  

- **Integrate Rendering Ecosystem v2.0**:  
  - reuse state machine invariants and failure modes,  
  - adapt them to analytical visualization instead of expressive rendering.  

> “Provide the PRECL-integrated successor to RESM v1.0. Update state definitions, transitions, invariants, failure modes, and acceptance conditions to ensure expressive-neutral rendering and RP-ALTITUDE protection…”  

- **Coordinate evolution**:  
  - versioning of dashboard specs,  
  - upgrades to visualization engines,  
  - addition of new panels and visualization types,  
  - maintenance of trauma‑informed constraints.

---

#### 4 — TISD‑specific justification

A Construction Suite is required **because**:

- TISD work is **survivor‑centered** and **trauma‑informed**.  
- Visualization must remain **analytical**, **neutral**, and **non‑expressive**.  
- Harm topology, burden‑shift lattices, and treaty‑layer collapse are **high‑stakes** structures—misrendering is itself a form of epistemic harm.  
- The Rendering Ecosystem already encodes the logic for **expressive‑neutral rendering** and **RP‑ALTITUDE protection**; the suite reuses this logic for TISD visualization.  

The justification document ensures:

- the Construction Suite is not “just a technical tool,”  
- but a **governance‑altitude commitment** to safe, coherent, trauma‑informed rendering.
