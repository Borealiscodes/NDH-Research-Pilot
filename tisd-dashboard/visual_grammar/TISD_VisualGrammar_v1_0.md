#### 2 — Core principles

- **Analytical only:** visuals represent structure (harm, topology, lattices), not emotion or narrative.  
- **Trauma‑informed:** palettes, density, and contrast avoid retraumatizing or overwhelming patterns.  
- **Invariant‑based:** rendering must satisfy altitude, geometry, palette, membrane, adjacency, and sequencing invariants.  
- **PRECL‑aligned:** expressive geometry and palettes collapse to neutral before rendering.  
- **Non‑activating:** no NDH geometry, no membranes, no governance adjacency, no runtime engines.

> “Provide the PRECL-integrated successor to RESM v1.0. Update state definitions, transitions, invariants, failure modes, and acceptance conditions to ensure expressive-neutral rendering and RP-ALTITUDE protection…”

#### 3 — Visualization primitives

- **Nodes:** represent domains (A/B/C/D/E), treaty layers, institutions, apex points.  
- **Edges:** represent harm flows, burden shifts, echo pathways, oversight failures.  
- **Lattices:** represent burden‑shifting structures and impunity circuits.  
- **Layers:** represent domain separation, treaty‑layer overlays, apex vacuums.  
- **Heat maps:** represent intensity of harm, echo density, oversight failure concentration.  
- **Topologies (SVG):** vector diagrams for harm‑topology, echo flows, lattice structures.  
- **Static renders (PNG):** snapshots of dashboards, heat maps, and topology states.

#### 4 — Palette and style rules (TSV‑aligned)

- **Base palette:** neutral greys for structure; no neon, no kawaii, no cosmic gradients.  
- **Domain accents:** muted, distinct colors per domain (A–E) to preserve separation.  
- **Treaty‑layer highlights:** reserved accent color for treaty‑layer failures and oversight collapse.  
- **Apex indicators:** minimal, high‑contrast markers for apex vacuums and burden‑shift apex.  
- **Heat map scale:** linear or log scale, trauma‑informed (no aggressive reds; use controlled warm/cool gradients).  

> “Validate gradient stops, apex binding, membrane-safe palettes.”

- **LSV_R — Membrane sovereignty**  
  - domain and treaty‑layer boundaries must remain visually distinct and non‑collapsed.

- **MTV_R — Sequencing traversal integrity**

#### 6 — Failure modes (visualization layer)

Rendering must abort if:

- **Altitude collapse:** visualization attempts governance or NDH‑CORE geometry.  
- **Membrane breach:** domain or treaty‑layer boundaries visually merge or blur beyond safe thresholds.  
- **Geometry drift:** harm‑topology diagrams distort structure (e.g., misleading edge routing).  
- **Palette contamination:** expressive/Kawaii or cosmic palettes re‑enter the pipeline.  
- **Provenance fracture:** visual output cannot be traced back to source artifacts (case study, matrix, map).  
- **Sequencing inversion:** visualization bypasses PRECL or invariant checks.  
- **Apex resonance leakage:** apex indicators become symbolic or emotionally charged.  
- **PRECL collapse failure:** expressive elements remain in the final render.
