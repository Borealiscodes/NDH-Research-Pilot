# ⭐ **3 — Triangulation Safety Rules (v1.0)**

Each begins with a Guided Link.

### **Rule M‑1 — Read‑Only Triangulation**  
RP‑altitude artifacts may *read* triangulation rules.  
They may not modify them.

### **Rule M‑2 — Freeze‑Seal Integrity**  
Triangulation lineage must remain sealed.

### **Rule M‑3 — ECC Adjacency Safety**  
ECC adjacency is visible but cannot be altered.

### **Rule M‑4 — Principle Spine Invariant Protection**  
Triangulation must not violate Principle Spine invariants.

### **Rule M‑5 — Treaty‑Layer Isolation**  
RP‑altitude artifacts cannot contaminate C0 altitude.

### **Rule M‑6 — Reversibility Inheritance**  
Triangulation interactions must remain reversible.

### **Rule M‑7 — Non‑Recursive Triangulation**  
Triangulation cannot reference future treaty‑layer artifacts.

### **Rule M‑8 — Construction Neutrality**  
Triangulation cannot activate geometry.
